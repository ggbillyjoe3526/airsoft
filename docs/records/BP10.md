## Part 1: bugs

Five reviewers read everything merged since 0.1 Beta 1 (`acfcb19..b0c5761`: respawns, Subway's second rework, the
Range's skyline and firing point, the Beta 2 interface, the train announcements) and a round of play with respawns
from hit to return. Every finding was checked against the code; these were real and are fixed, each with a test:

- **A round won while you wait to come back wasn't yours.** With respawns a player hit and waiting is still on the
  team, but the round's XP (`PlayerTally`) and the scoreboard's rounds won (`MatchStats.rounds_won_with`) only counted
  those in play. Both count a waiting player now; one life is as before.
- **Coming back** faced the way you looked when hit, with toggled aim and crouch still on; it now faces the spawn
  (`PlayerInput.face`). The "What got you" card and the kept hit lines from your hit stayed up
  after you were back in play; your own return takes them down (a teammate's doesn't).
- **The view while out** jumped to whichever teammate came back first (the lowest id); it now stays on the teammate
  you were watching while they are in play (`PlaySession._out_watch_id`).
- **The round table** showed "Hit by …" and dimmed a player who was hit and came back; it shows "Still in" for anyone
  in play at the end, and dims only those still out.
- **Bots called "Last one" and "Down, N left"** from a count that left out the players waiting to come back; with
  respawns open there is no such count, so they don't call it (`Callouts._on_hit`).
- **A bot coming back under Elimination's overtime flag** never reacted to it (its overtime time stayed at infinity
  from the reset); it gets its own reaction time and flank pick as a bot in play did (`BotController._rejoin`).
- **The loadout card menu** opened under the first card when right-clicked on another card: carrying the card
  rebuilt the strip before the menu read where it was. It now stands where the card was clicked, and from the keys
  (the Menu key) it hangs under the card and keeps the keyboard's focus there, after a Duplicate too.
- **"You have the bomb"** was told again every time a plant was let go of (the line keyed on carrying, which a plant
  in hand set off and on). It is told once a pick-up and keeps its own time across a plant.
- **Subway's announcements** followed whoever the camera watched (a bot in a train set them off) and could start over
  the match's results; they are the player's own, in play, and a new match cuts one still playing. DECISIONS says the
  egg is the player's.
- **The tunnel grille** had no line in `docs/ASSETS.md`; the train build script named a material by number where every
  other part names it (`tools/blender/maps/train.py`; not run, the cloud has no Blender, and the models are as built).

Two new guards play Plant The Bomb with respawns on Woodland and Subway (CI's `respawn-plant-guards`); Depot's was in.

**The flag's slip was kept as measured.** A reviewer read RS1's rule (an untended flag slides back) as meant for a
part-raised flag only. Letting a fully raised flag stand gave Depot's attackers 55 of 62 rounds (88.7 %, 8 seeds, band
40 to 65), and RS1 had tuned every defender wait with it sliding, so the code is unchanged and the comments, the wiki
and a test say what it does: with respawns an attacker stays on the rope through the hold.

## Decisions

- **The flag slips when raised too** (above): RS1's measured rule, now written down.
- **Bots make no count of who is left while respawns are open**: anyone hit may come back, so a count would be wrong.
- **The defenders' wait scales with the bots' level** (above), by the other team's level when your teammates play
  another: a match has one level for its records too.
- **XP a match is left higher with respawns**: XP a minute is unchanged, and the owner sets the progression.
