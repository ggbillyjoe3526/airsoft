## What changed

The human figure's four heads, rebuilt from the owner's photos (`ref_images.zip` › `clothes/highcut_helmet`,
`baseball_cap`, `boonie_hat`, `goggles`, `mesh_face_mask`) by new scripts in `tools/blender/figures/` and spliced into
`assets/models/characters/human.glb` (its other nodes untouched):

- **Head_HighCut:** a FAST-style grey high cut (photos 1-5): superellipsoid shell with the ear cut-out's S-curve,
  rubber edge trim, oval vents, a slotted rail each side, the front shroud with its mount window and bolts, loop
  panels over the top, each side and round the back (the team patch on it), ear defenders on the rails with a boom
  mic, the chin harness; a steel mesh mask and glasses under it.
- **Head_FullCut:** the same shell carried down over the ears in a coyote cover, rails, shroud, loop panels and the
  team band; goggles under the brim, their strap under the shell's edge.
- **Head_Cap:** a coyote tactical cap (photos 3 and 4): six panels, a structured front, the front loop panel (the
  team patch on it) and side panels, laser-cut side-rear panels, the button, a stitched curved bill, the snap strap;
  goggles under the bill, the mesh mask, a headset over the crown.
- **Head_Boonie:** an olive boonie (boonie_olive_3): a soft crown with dents and a pinched top, eyelets, a stitched
  brim that drops at the front and back, the team band with six foliage loops, the chin cord and toggle; a sand
  scarf over the nose and glasses.
- Every head has LOD1 and LOD2, each about 95 to 100 % of the agreed 8,000 / 2,600 / 800 (the largest 7,996 /
  2,592 / 794 triangles). One 2048 px atlas (`human_headwear_col`, `_nrm`, `_orm`) painted with numpy: knit with the
  eyes, loop, mesh, ripstop, stitching, shells, straps. The lens is clear with a slight smoke (`Lens`, alpha
  blended), so the eyes show.
- `human.glb` 4.0 MB to 6.4 MB (the atlas and the denser heads).

## Decisions

- No full-cut photos: the full cut is the high-cut shell carried down over the ears, in a coyote cover (navy is too
  close to Alpha's blue).
- No shooting-glasses photos: the glasses use the goggles' lens shape with arms.
- Unbranded throughout (the goggle photo's logo and print left off).
- The combinations of the v12 heads are kept (high cut with mask and glasses, full cut with goggles, cap with mask,
  goggles and headset, boonie with scarf and glasses), and so are their team marks.
- The goggle strap is dark grey, not the photo's near white, so it doesn't read as a pale block or a mark.
- The coyote is tuned against the photos under the game's own field lighting (AgX and its grade), not in Blender, so
  Blender renders are a shade darker than the photos.
- The mask's top follows the lower edge of the eyewear worn with it, so the lens sits over it as on a player.
- The atlas `.import` files keep Godot's `generator_parameters` md5: with it Godot re-extracts the atlas when the
  `.glb` changes (an emptied one kept the first paint).
- Every head and level uses 95 to 100 % of its budget (owner, 2026-11-05): `DENSITY` in `headwear.py` scales each
  head's counts per level (the boonie's LOD1 stops at 2,432, the next step goes over).
- No wiki page describes the figures' gear, so no wiki change.
