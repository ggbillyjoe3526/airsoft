## What changed

The human figure's `Body` (and `Body_LOD1`, `Body_LOD2`) is rebuilt by the scripts in `tools/blender/figures/` from
the owner's photos (`ref_images.zip` › `player/figure`, `clothes`, `hands/gloves`). The skeleton (65 bones), the 32
clips, the heads, the carrier and gear are untouched: the new body is spliced into `human.glb` by node with
`splice_part.py` (the pads thread's splicer).

- **Anatomy** (`anatomy.py`, `source/`): the MakeHuman base mesh (CC0) from the neck down, shaped as a young man,
  moved onto the figure's skeleton at rest by per-bone affine moves blended over the joints. It is never drawn bare;
  the clothes are cut from it and offset.
- **Clothes** (`body.py`, `garment.py`, `details.py`): a combat shirt (folds modelled into the sleeves: stacked above
  the cuffs, creased in the elbows, draped from the armpits; a darker torso under the carrier, olive raglan sleeves, a
  mock-neck collar with a zip and pull, shoulder and elbow pockets, flat cuffs with velcro tabs, the team armbands);
  camouflage trousers (waistband, fuller thighs tapering to the boot, bloused over the boots with stacked folds, thigh
  cargo pockets turned to the front, calf pockets, slash pockets, fly, seat and knee seams).
- **Gloves** (`details.py`): dark gloves with a moulded knuckle shell in one piece (a dome per knuckle) on a lighter
  leather border, domed finger guards, a palm reinforcement and a wide cuff with a velcro strap, from the owner's glove
  photos.
- **Boots** (`boots.py`): coyote mid boots lofted through contours (56 points each), a darker rubber sole with a lip,
  heel step, arch, a midsole band and a dark tread edge, a toe cap, a heel counter, a tongue, crossed laces, eyelets and
  speed hooks.
- **Textures** (`bake.py`): one 2K set (colour, normal, ORM) baked in Blender. The normal map is baked from a
  subdivided copy with the folds displaced, with the seams and stitches as bump. Colour is baked from per-region
  materials (brushed four-tone camouflage, the raglan split by position) under the body's own occlusion.
- **Detail** (owner, 2026-11-05: "needs to be higher quality with more detail"): press studs on the pocket flaps, a
  velcro field on the shoulder pocket, back pockets and belt loops; a ripstop weave and fine grain in the normal map;
  wear on the elbows, knees and seat and dust on the hems and boots in the colour; the chest smoothed flat under the
  shirt; the lower legs subdivided, with creases modelled behind the knees. A model uses about 95 % of its triangle
  budget (owner, 2026-11-05): the gloves are at twice the hand's density.
- **Weights:** the trousers' hem hangs from the shins (no foot weights), and the weights round the knees are blurred,
  so kneeling no longer tears the cloth beside where the v12 knee pads were.
- **Hit areas:** `tools/hit_fit_tune.gd` was rerun on the new body with GA1's running clips, and its numbers are in
  `HitConfig`'s `fit_*`, except `fit_thigh_radius` (0.085, not the tuner's 0.090) and `fit_chest_back` (0.078 with
  BL11's heads, not 0.082): the tuner's values let more than 5 % of BBs beside a vaulting figure strike it
  (test_hit_registration's bar).
- **Materials:** `Clothing` and `TeamCloth`. The armbands are painted in the team colour by the game.
- **Budget:** body 33,458 triangles at LOD0 (its share is 34,000), 10,999 at LOD1, 3,298 at LOD2. The whole figure
  with the largest of BL11's heads is 46,574 at LOD0 (60,000 budget). `tests/unit/test_figure_body.gd`'s counts and
  the sidecar's parts (`Kit_Pads`) are updated.

## Decisions

- **Split across threads (2026-11-05):** each part of the figure has one owner and a triangle share, spliced in by
  node (`docs/DECISIONS.md` › The human figure is built in parts).
- **Interim knee pads.** Until BL13 merges, the v12 knee pads are cut out of the v12 body as `Kit_Pads`, so the figure
  keeps its pads.
- **Defaults** (owner rule: never wait, take the default and say so):
  - Team colour on the carrier and the armbands only.
  - Sleeves solid olive `667052`, matching the first-person arms; torso `58533f`.
  - Four-tone camouflage trousers.
  - Dark gloves (`4c4638`, guards `242422`), because coyote gloves read as bare skin on the figure (the arms thread was
    told).
- **Weights** are transferred from the v12 body (the clips were tuned on it), so the hit areas fitted to the bones in
  HV1 still hold.

## Known issues left

  `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`).
- The v12 knee pads stay as `Kit_Pads` until BL13 merges (`docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`).
- Size: `human.glb` grows from 4.0 MB to 7.0 MB, and the textures Godot extracts beside it add 1.5 MB, well inside the
  500 MB install budget.
- The Rifle_Aim support hand and the running clips belong to the animation work (the owner asked for new running
  animations; that is its own thread).
