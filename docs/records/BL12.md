## What changed

The human figure's gear (`Kit`, `Kit_Pack`, `Kit_Radio` and their LODs in `assets/models/characters/human.glb`), rebuilt
from the owner's reference photos (`ref_images.zip` › `player/plate_carrier`, `pouches`, `backpack`, `radio`):

- **Plate carrier** (team colour): JPC-style front and back plate bags, pillowed, with a raised binding rim; a loop
  panel and a MOLLE row on the front, five MOLLE rows and a webbing drag handle on the back; padded shoulder straps
  with large side-release buckles; a cummerbund either side with three MOLLE rows.
- **Pouches** (coyote): three tall open-top magazine pouches after the Viper-style photo (two webbing bands, a bound
  mouth, the magazine 3.5 cm proud, a shock cord over its base plate to a pull tab with a ring); a lidded utility
  pouch on the right of the cummerbund.
- **Belt:** at the waist (0.94 to 1.0 m, the owner's review), a padded battle belt with a tan rigger belt through it,
  a MOLLE row and a metal buckle; an empty polymer holster with an open mouth on a short drop mount on the right hip,
  sized to take either pistol (40 mm inside, 88 mm over the trigger guard: the Cyber Pistol, the larger, is 38 and 84
  mm, the GBB Pistol 30 and 71 mm); a lidded grenade pouch on the left front; a rolled dump pouch at the back.
- **Radio:** a dual-band handheld radio in a snug pouch on the left of the cummerbund, with a 6 cm stub antenna
  leaning back clear of the arm (the owner allowed a short one or none).
- **Pack** (ranger green): a main compartment curved to the back plate, with zip piping; a lower pocket (0.58 of its
  height) with MOLLE and an admin pocket above it with a loop panel, each with its own zip; two compression straps a
  side with buckles; a grab handle; shoulder straps over the carrier's ending in ladder locks.
- **Triangles:** 13,872 / 4,374 / 1,204 at LOD0 / LOD1 / LOD2 (the gear's share of the figure's 60k / 20k / 6k,
  agreed with BL10 and BL11: 14,000 / 4,500 / 1,500). No decimation: the build stops if LOD0 runs over.
- **Textures:** one 256 x 32 swatch strip per family (`human_gear_col`, `human_gear_orm`, `human_teamgear_col`, the
  last multiplied by the team colour like the armband).
- **Built by** `tools/blender/figure-gear/gear.py` (with `fit.py`, which fits the gear to the body by ray casts),
  spliced into `human.glb` by node name (`splice.py`) so the body, heads and clips are untouched.
- **Fitted to the body:** the plates and the pack are drawn in to the torso by ray casts (`fit.plate_inset`, a 6 mm
  gap, 2 cm under the cummerbund's edges), so the carrier no longer stands off the body; the shoulder straps follow.
- **Fitted to it:** the hit volume's chest and hip shapes (HitConfig `fit_*`, retuned by `tools/hit_fit_tune.gd`; the
  back capsule now has its own radius and ends, `fit_chest_back_radius`, `_low`, `_high`, for the pack), the
  slung rifle's clearance over the pack (`sling_back_pack` 0.27 to 0.34 m) and the figure's triangle test.

## Decisions

- Unbranded throughout; the carrier, its straps and webbing are in the team colour (multiplied, as the armband),
  the pouches coyote, the pack ranger green: the owner's photos mix colours, and the team colour must read from the
  front.
- The holster is empty (the owner, 2026-11-05: "just the model of the holster itself"), and one holster takes both
  pistols, sized to the larger, the Cyber Pistol, so it suits whichever the figure carries.
- The radio on the left of the cummerbund, behind the arm (the old kit's place, the photos' common one).
- The critic's strap facets and strap loops were left: no triangles left for them, and the critic did not block on
  them.
- The pack slimmed to 10.5 cm deep, its top lowered to 1.39 m and tapered, so the aim-down pose's hit volume still covers it.
- Robots wear the human's fit, so the gear's larger back capsule raises their thin air: its bar (`ROBOT_BAR_THIN`)
  rises with it (robots are Dev only and get no further work).
- LOD2 drops the holster, grenade and dump pouches (the budget); the right hip's silhouette is simpler at range.

## Known issues

- The long dark cord under the left arm in the owner's review is the old (v12) body's own radio cord, not the gear's;
  the new body (BL10) has none.

- In the crouch and aim poses the forearm passes through the chest's front, as it did with the old kit (the clips',
  not the gear's).
