## Bugs

Two review agents (Opus) read every line the seven merges changed, one the simulation, bots, navigation and data, one
the HUD, menus, views and shaders, with the known issues named so they wouldn't report them again. Neither found a bug
a player meets often; two small ones are fixed, each with a test:

- **A walk-off hidden in leaves or smoke still counted as seen.** Since TV1 a hit player whose respawn wait is running
  out walks on (up to 3 s) while anyone in play can see them, so nobody watches them fade. `Respawns.seen` asked only
  the level's sight ray, so a bush or a smoke cloud between them hid nothing and their respawn came up to 3 s late.
  It now also counts the leaves and smoke on the line (`HitConfig.seen_leaves` 0.6 m, none within `seen_leaves_near`
  2.5 m, and `seen_smoke` 0.8 m: the bots' own sight rules), with the round's smoke handed over by `Simulation.step`.
- **The Remove panel's two links sat off its middle** (UB3): every link adds a gap after itself, so Keep stood 16 px
  further from the panel's right edge than Remove from its left. The row's last gap is dropped.

Latent, not changed: `LoadoutStrip._show_confirm`'s branch with no menu up places the panel from a card laid out a
moment later; nothing in the game asks Remove without the menu, only tests do. A stale comment in
`GraphicsGuard.try_again` (haze levels) is corrected.

Checked and fine (both agents, traced): zero and short respawn waits, the bomb armed mid-walk, a round's end mid-fade,
walk-offs in lifts, the climb-drop routes on maps without drops, `drops_reach` over cycles, the thicket tiers' shape
owners, the lift's sound lead, the settings file's version 3 to 7 haze rows, the Remove panel's focus and re-entry,
every prompt's words and rebinds, the Ultra underground light switch, the new materials' prewarm lists and missing
textures.

**GPU on Low** (software Vulkan, relative only, 960 x 540; `pipeline/runs/BP12/woodland-low/` in the shared files):
Subway's three views are no slower than at BP11 (536 / 452 / 352 ms against 573 / 549 / 390). Woodland's take about

simpler levels come only at errors of metres (an oak's crown at 3.07 m), so a far crown draws every card. Two levers

and far fill). Both change the look William approved, so neither ships; the figures are a known issue and on his Low
check (N47), with the prototype kept beside them.
