## Decisions

- **Sniper Rifle: unchanged (owner).** His new `sniper_rifle_shot` was tried twice in samples
  (`audio/william/preview/au10/`): with the pistol's crack laid on it and its run-up 4 dB down (his first ask), then
  his first shot with the new one's twang laid under it (his second ask). He then said "keep the old sniper sound", so
  `shot_spring` and `power_spring` are byte for byte as before; the new file is kept unused.
- **Compact SMG: the stand-ins, with his toothbrush's whir (owner).** His `smg_firing` bursts are clean once
  high-passed at 300 Hz (about 30 dB over the room), but the room's steady hum (`smg_room_tone`, the same hum) is as
  loud as the motor's low end. He preferred the stand-in shot and magazine, and asked for "a bit of a whir of the
  electric motor from maybe my toothbrush"; `power_smg` is the AEG motor a fifth up with his toothbrush 4 dB under it
  (`SMG_BRUSH_DB`), and "keep ... the new smg sound in the sample you just gave me" confirmed it. The SMG cues are no
  longer labelled placeholders (his pick); `shot_smg` and the magazines are as before.
- **Metal is one new set.** His new walk, run and jumps replace his first walk (`walking_metal`, which needed a
  250 Hz high-pass for its rumble) for the run and sprint too, so the three come from one floor. Default.
- **Soil and water are his.** `step_earth_*` (was gravel softened) and `step_water_*` (was `tools/water_sounds.py`'s
  splashes): water at the splashes' levels and not balanced with the dry grounds (it is meant to carry further), each
  splash ringing longer than a dry step (0.45, 0.32 and 0.9 s). The BB plop (`impact_water`) stays synthesised;
  `tools/water_sounds.py` now makes only it (its takes re-rolled, same recipe, as the splashes' draws are gone).
- **The lift.** `lift_doors` is his three door takes (3 s each, the slide swelling and dying away) for both opening and
  shutting; `lift_motor` his two rides (`up_down`) from the motor's start (after the first ride's chime) for 3.2 s,
  faded before the car's stop. Both at the placeholders' loudness. The chime at the start of his first ride sits only
  about 10 dB over the car's hum, so the chime, button and buzzer stay `tools/lift_sounds.py`'s; `movement` (the
  steady hum in the car) is kept unused for a longer ride if one is needed. Default.
- **Subway's background.** A new ambience `tunnel` (`AmbienceConfig.IDS`, `play_tuning.tres`, `subway.json` through
  `tools/subway/build_map.py`): his tunnel as a 46 s loop, its phone rumble under 60 Hz taken out, at Depot's bed gain
  (0.03), with the creak call it had while it borrowed Depot's set (every set has a call).
- **Not over-processed:** high-passes for rumble and hum only; no pitch or clipping on his new takes.

## Known issues left
