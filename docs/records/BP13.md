## The harsher critic

`.claude/agents/critic.md` runs at high effort and accepts only at 8 of 8 (`docs/DECISIONS.md`): a check passes only
on cited evidence, at least five edge cases are traced through the changed code (round end mid-action, respawn,
pause, teardown, missing data, a second press, one tick for two, the slowest preset), and a list of hard fails needs
no judgment (an untyped variable, a magic number, a test that passes without the feature, a tuning change with no
figures, a ruled look changed, a useful file deleted). No near-miss re-run and no auto-accept: `tools/review_line.sh`
now takes only `critic Accept 8/8`.

## Bugs

Two review agents (Opus) read every line merged since BP12 (#405 to #410) and swept the game for rare cases, one the
simulation, bots and data, one the menus, input, settings and sounds. Fixed, each with a test (`test_bp13_qa.gd`):

- **Alt+Tab behind cover stood you up.** With crouch on toggle, any press of the sprint key (Alt) dropped the crouch,
  so Alt+Tab, Alt+Enter or AltGr did too. Running forward still stands you up (PC1's sprint-first); a press standing
  still no longer does.
- **A ping right after a Z tap was lost.** During the tap's 0.3 s the wheel counts as open, so a middle-click there was
  dropped; since PC1 the view turns during the tap, so the ping lands where you look and is taken.
- **An action bound to Enter switched the window.** Alt is the sprint and Alt+Enter the fullscreen key, so Enter
  pressed on the run went to the window. Enter and keypad Enter are unbindable now (an older save's Enter binding goes
  back to its default).
- **A window could be dragged too small to read.** The window stops at 960 x 540 (`DisplayConfig.min_window`).
- **Bots counted teammates on another storey as at the spot.** The crowd cap and the hunters' fan-out (BG1) measured
  across the ground only, so on Subway two teammates on the concourse kept the rest from a fight on the platform under
  them. Where the nav grid stacks floors under a teammate, the floor they stand on lying more than
  `BotConfig.spread_storey_rise` (2 m) from the floor under the spot's height is another storey; a slope is one floor,
  so Woodland's hills count as before (attempt 1's height test misread 9.5 % of 12 m pairs there: critic).
- **A lift's shut begun from ajar hung still.** With someone aboard in the doorway the countdown held at the full shut
  time, so leaves standing half open waited out the whole of it before closing; they now wait from where they stand.

Checked and fine (traced by the agents): the What got you settings from versions 1 to 7, XP at 1.5x and saves from
before it, the red HIT! sign's prewarm, LS1's step-in and heard-in-car guards, same-tick wipes in every mode, the full
respawn reset, the bomb defender column's levels, WH1's tier bodies on a cancelled load, seeding (no unseeded
randomness in the simulation or bots).

## The storey rule's bot figures

A teammate is on another storey when their floor and the spot's lie more than 2 m apart and the line between them
meets a drop or climb of more than 2 m from one cell to the next (attempt 3; attempt 1 used raw height, which split
Woodland's hills, and attempt 2 the stacking under the teammate alone, which missed a concourse edge: critic). Over
200,000 random pairs of walkable floor points up to 12 m apart a map (seed 7, `pipeline/runs/BP13/balance/pairs3.gd.txt`
in the shared files), of the pairs more than 2 m apart in height: Subway 87,468, of which 1,378 (1.6 %, its stairs)
still count together; Woodland 11,762, of which 11,549 (98.2 %, its hills) still count together; Depot none. No pair
gives a different answer either way round. So Depot plays exactly as before, Woodland all but so.

## Performance

- **Input latency.** Mouse look is applied per event and read every frame, presses are latched between ticks and the
  command filled before each step: no added delay beyond the 60 Hz tick. V-Sync on is FIFO, which queues two or three
  frames; Mailbox was tried (attempt 1) and put back, as it lifts the frame cap V-Sync on gives the players who turn
  it on for smoothness and a cool laptop (critic).
- **Loading.** Starting the nav grid's worker task with the map, beside the level's build, was tried and put back:
  headless, idle machine, two runs each, Woodland 7.19 / 6.64 s against 7.10 / 6.06 s (inside the spread), the longest
  loading frame 226 / 176 ms against 394 / 279 ms, and Esc during the level step waited for the grid (known issue).
- **Frame pacing.** Prewarm measured on Subway's Plant The Bomb (software Vulkan, empty shader cache): no draw-time
  pipeline compiles over 600 frames of play after the load. CPU (`tools/team_bench.gd`, 6v6, 45 s, ms mean / p95 /
  p99 / worst on a busy machine): Woodland Plant The Bomb 4.36 / 6.39 / 9.02 / 24.29, Attack / Defend 4.70 / 8.03 /
  12.33 / 29.86; Subway Plant The Bomb 4.92 / 6.57 / 10.49 / 31.50, Attack / Defend 4.12 / 5.49 / 8.11 / 15.59; no
  regression against BP12. No per-frame allocation found in the play session, the HUD's text or the simulation.

## Models, textures and animations

An asset audit (269 models, 91 images, import settings) found every model inside its budget (figure 59.3k of 60k,
replicas 37.8k to 40.0k, arms 38.9k, attachments 9,980 at most), no texture over 2K, every normal map flagged, every
3D texture mipmapped and the animations already optimised (38.9k glTF keys to 13.2k). Changed, with no visible
difference:

- The Compact SMG and the Pump Shotgun imported with generated levels of detail and as static for baked light, unlike
  every other replica (BP5): on Low a simpler mesh could show in your hands, and on Ultra the scene GI voxelised them.
  Both now import as the others (`generate_lods=false`, `light_baking=0`), 0.92 MB of index data less.
- The loading screen's picture is drawn with its mipmaps, so it shrinks smoothly in a window smaller than it.

Left for the owner or later (known issues): the arms' roughness maps at 1024 (about 6 MB, wants a render against
2048 first) and their baked-light import (both arms changes), the bare-hand maps loaded for a Dev view, the figures'
double-sided materials.

## Replicas and parts

Every replica's numbers were worked out from the data by the game's own ballistics and accuracy code, and every
combination of power source, barrel, optic, grip, laser, silencer and magazine (AEG 864, Sniper 72, Compact SMG 32,
GBB 24, Pump Shotgun 24) scored on 16 axes: none beats or matches another on all of them. The LiPo on the AEG is the
nearest to a must-have (+15 % rate for +30 % kick) and stays, within RP1's bench noise. Changed:

- **The GBB Pistol's Moving spread 1 to 0.8** (`data/stats.md` and the built-in `game/config/replicas/pistol.tres`, which must match it: CI): the Cyber Pistol was tighter than it in every stance,
  the run included (1.26° against 1.35°), so the run cost the Cyber nothing against the other pistol. The GBB's run
  spread is now 1.19°, under the Cyber's, still above the AEG's 1.17°; the Cyber's owner ruling is untouched. Bots
  don't draw sidearms, so the bot figures don't move.
- The Pump Shotgun joins `test_rp1_balance.gd`'s trade-off checks; `tests/balance/sniper_bench.gd` reads the kit table
  again (its rows lacked the Grenades and Fields columns); `data/stats.md`'s silencer note names the Compact SMG, which
  takes one.
- Shotgun bots pull to 29 m, past its 24 m reach; holding them to 24 m waits for a bot filter (known issue).

## Decisions

- **A press of the sprint key alone never stands a toggled crouch up,** and **Enter can't be bound** while Alt is the
  sprint and Alt+Enter the fullscreen key (`docs/DECISIONS.md` › Gameplay and feel).
- **A teammate counts at a spot only on its storey,** by the nav grid's stacked floors (`BotConfig.spread_storey_rise`,
  a field of its own, not the anchors' `anchor_search_rise`).
