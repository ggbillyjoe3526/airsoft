## What changed

- **The owner's ask** (2026-11-10): rework the levels for the Compact SMG, cap 15 or 20. The thread recommended 15 now
  and 20 or more with the Progression Update, when skins can fill the levels; he said go ahead.
- **Levels** (`data/levels.md`): the cap goes from 10 to 15. A replica every five levels: the Sniper Rifle at 5, the
  Compact SMG at 10 (dev content, so level 10 also gives the public Variable Scope) and the Cyber Pistol at 15. The
  other items are spread so every level gives one or two: Tight-Bore Barrel 6, Angled Grip and Low-Cap Magazine 7, Red
  Gas 8, M140 Spring 9, LiPo battery 11, Long Barrel 12, Black Gas 13, M150 Spring 14.
- **XP.** Levels 1 to 10 keep their XP (6,500 in all), so no save changes level and none loses an unlock (unlocks are
  never taken away). Levels 11 to 15 take 1,400, 1,500, 1,600, 1,700 and 1,800: 14,500 in all, about 58 first-to-5
  matches on Normal and 39 on Hard (at about 250 XP a Normal match).
- **On the Compact SMG** (CS1, #283, merged first at level 7): rebased, it moves to level 10 beside the Variable
  Scope; its test, the CS1 DECISIONS entry, FEATURES and its changelog note follow.
- No code changes: the table is data, and the Armory, XP card and saves already follow its rows.
- Tests that pinned the old levels now pin the new ones (or the cap); QA added `test_lv15_qa.gd` (an old cap-10 save
  keeps its level and unlocks, every level gives something public). DECISIONS, ROADMAP, PLAYTEST, the wiki's Levels
  and Unlocks and Replicas pages are updated. The play check (the Armory with 15 levels, an old save, the pace) is on
  the owner's Dev 3 checklist in the project files, `playtest/0.1-dev3-checklist.md`, with Armory pictures in
  `playtest/levels-lv15/`.

## Defaults taken

- 15, not 20: 21 items to unlock after level 0; 20 levels would mean single small parts per level and over 100 matches.
- Levels 1 to 10 keep their XP so existing saves keep their level.
- The SMG at 10, not 8, for the five-level rhythm (the owner was offered 8).
