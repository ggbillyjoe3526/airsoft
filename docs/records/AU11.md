## Goal

The owner, 2026-11-13, with 7 recordings (`audio/william/raw/plant-the-bomb/` and `shotgun/` in the shared project
files): "I've attached sounds. For the shotgun's shot sound reuse the sniper rifle shot sound. for the bomb
placement/dropping sound make it quieter."

## Decisions

- **Which file is which.** By name and by what is in each: `placing_bomb` (three takes of a box set down: a light
  touch, a thump, a lighter knock) is the bomb put down; `button_press` (seven keys, each a 2 kHz beep and a click as
  it is let go) the code keyed in; `bomb_timer` (a timer: 15 beeps a second apart, then quick ones) the fuse's
  beep; `defusing_bomb` (bursts of clicks, most with a 5.2 kHz beep, then a click, a one-second beep and a click)
  the defusal and, its last take, the bomb made safe; `bomb_explosion` (six seconds of a burst fading out) going off;
  `shotgun_reload` (two strokes in 0.6 s) the pump; `shotgun_shell_load` (five takes, each a pop and a click a quarter
  second later) the shell pulled off and pushed home. Defaults: `button_press` is the plant's keys, not the lift's
  button (the lift's stays synthesised); the defusal's last take is "made safe".
- **The shotgun's shot is the Sniper Rifle's (owner).** `shot_shotgun` and a new `power_shotgun` are `shot_spring`
  and `power_spring` byte for byte (the spring's run-up is part of the rifle's shot as heard; it plays at the default
  power gain, the spring's 2.5). The pitched-down thump and `SHOTGUN_SHOT_PITCH`, `SHOTGUN_PUMP_PITCH` are gone.
- **Put down and picked up quieter (owner).** `SoundConfig.plant_handle_gain` 0.7 to 0.35, 6 dB, for both: his
  set-down is a loud, close thump. The cues stay at the stand-ins' level, so the gain is the one dial.
- **Picked up is a stand-in.** He recorded no pick-up: each set-down's lighter second knock (0.3 s, 10 % higher)
  stands in, labelled a placeholder (`--placeholders`), in `audio/record-next.md`. Default.
- **Armed stays synthesised** (nothing recorded for it); `tools/plant_sounds.py` now makes only `plant_armed_1`,
  byte for byte as before.
- **Fitted to the game's timings, not re-shaped.** The keys: each of his seven keys 0.5 s from just before its beep,
  back to back, so they fill the 3.5 s plant (his were 0.7 to 2 s apart over 7 s). The defusal: 6.0 s from its
  first burst (the defuse's length; unit test). The fuse's beep: his first beep's first 0.11 s (its tone, not its
  ring), under the fuse's fastest gap of 0.12 s (unit test). The set-down from 0.03 s before its thump (his hand's
  touch before it is left out, so the thump lands with the event). The pump: his two strokes moved to where the
  model's Fire clip has the pump back and home, as the sniper's bolt is (three variants, a few per cent apart in
  pitch). The shell: each take split at the quietest point between the pop and the click.
- **Levels.** Each at its stand-in's loudest 50 ms, so SoundConfig's gains hold; the keys and the beep 4.5 and
  0.9 dB under (the stand-ins were square waves; his are as loud as they go under the peak cap without a limiter).
  No pitch, clipping or extra filtering on his takes beyond the script's usual 90 Hz high-pass.
