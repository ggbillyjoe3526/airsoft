# Features

What the game has on `main` today, by area: one line per feature, with the ids that added it. The changelog agent keeps
this file (`.claude/agents/changelog.md`).

Depot is playable from the title screen against four stand-in figures (FP1). The browser game's list,
`legacy/browser/docs/FEATURES.md`, is the parity list: each line is a feature to rebuild, change on the owner's word,
or drop (`docs/ROADMAP.md` › What 0.1 is).

## Simulation rules

- BB flight: BBs fly with drag, gravity and hop-up lift; the AEG Rifle stays on target to about 38 m, the GBB Pistol to about 26 m (FP1)
- Hit calling: one hit eliminates; you raise an arm, call "HIT!" and walk off to the dead zone round cover, where you watch through a teammate's eyes; bots call their hits honestly (FP1, BE1)
- Loadouts: up to five named loadouts, one carried into each match; each replica has its own BB weight (0.20 to 0.30 g), hop-up dial, colour and power source, with batteries setting the AEG Rifle's rate of fire and gas the GBB Pistol's power, capped at the field's chrono limit; bots keep their replicas as they come (LO1)
- Replica colours: your replica wears the colours picked in your loadout, in your hands; bots hold theirs (Alpha in Cobalt and Onyx, Beta in Signal and Coral), and the Cyber Pistol keeps its own; Realistic colours shows bold schemes as plain colours from the next match, and turns the Cyber Pistol plain grey with no glow (LO3, CP1)
- Holster: press 3 to holster the replica (out of view, "Holstered" panel, no crosshair); holstered you run faster than with it in hand, never faster than a sprint, and sprint a tenth faster; a trigger pull, aim, reload or slot key draws it again, slower than a normal switch; bots holster on long calm runs (MO1)
- Elimination rounds: first to 5 against the clock, with ends swapped at half time; a finished match rematches 15 s after its summary shows, or on a click (FP1, SM1)
- Attack / Defend flag rules: attackers raise the flag at the defenders' pole in 5 s, defenders pull it down in 3 s, and both teams at the pole hold it still; a raised flag wins for the attackers, a time-out for the defenders, with up to 15 s of overtime while attackers are on the rope (FG1)
- Overtime flag in Elimination: when the clock runs out with both teams in, a flag goes up at the map's spot; the first team to touch it wins the round, two teams at it hold each other off, a wipe-out still wins, and 30 s untouched is a draw played again; bots react by level, run for it (some round a flank), anchors and careful bots watch it from cover first, and each team's nearest bot calls it; the chequered flag rises at the spot with a reach ring, the clock shows OT and the flag's time, a banner and three short whistle blasts mark it, the marker and minimap show it, and a round won there ends FLAG TOUCHED (OV1, OV2, OV3)
- Attack / Defend in play: pick it on the Match screen (Depot or Woodland, maps with a flagpole), with a flagpole and reach ring at the defenders' flag spot, the flag climbing in the attackers' colour, a FLAG gauge with 12 notches, ATK and DEF tags, OT on the clock, the "Their flag is going up!" warning, a "FLAG · 24 m" marker, round banners and XP and records as Elimination (FG3)
- Replicas: AEG Rifle (semi, burst, auto), GBB Pistol (semi) and Cyber Pistol (semi, burst, auto; level 10; built-in battery, CP1), magazines swapped on reload, the GBB Pistol's slide locks back on empty until the reload seats a magazine (MZ1), a draw on switching, light recoil and spread that grows when moving or jumping and tightens when crouching, holding still or aiming down sights, read from `data/stats.md` (FP1)
- Replica parts: red dots, scopes, grips, hi-cap and low-cap magazines, barrels, silencers and the pistol laser change how a replica plays; parts are picked on the Customise screen and show on the replica in your hands (AT1, AT2, AT3)
- Wind: each match has a light breeze (calm to about 2 m/s, with gusts), new every match; it pushes BBs, never players, and dust specks drift with it (BB1)
- Hop-up dial: 4 (less) and 5 (more), or Page Down and Page Up, turn each replica's dial for the match; the replica panel shows the setting and flat-shot range; bots keep factory dials (BB1)
- Ricochets: BBs bounce off concrete and steel containers; by default a bounced hit doesn't count ("Ricochet: play on"), and a match rule can make it count, not yet in a menu (BB1)
- Movement: walk, run, sprint, crouch, a limited jump (FP1), lean with Q or E (slows to a walk; eases back when a wall cuts it short) and vault over low walls with Jump, with "Space to vault" under the crosshair (BE1, MO1)
- HUD: navy and yellow look matching the menus; top bar with each team's score as pips toward the win, round clock, round number and FIRST TO 5; slanted banners for round start, half time with ends swapped, and who took the round or match; bottom right replica panel with BB count, fire-mode chips (S, B, A) with the current one lit, a box per magazine (loaded first, then fullest spare), HOLSTERED, RELOADING, LAST MAG or OUT OF BBS, and a low magazine turns yellow; a crosshair that opens with your spread (FP1, MZ1, HD1)
- Hit feed and teammate markers: a hit feed in the top right shows who fired, the BB trail and who was hit with its HIT call, and your own hit stays framed in it until the next round; teammates wear a chevron that shows through walls and greys out when hit (HF1, WG1)
- What got you card: when your hit is called, a card on the left shows the shooter's side, distance, held angle, time in view and whether you were moving, when known; the HUD tab's What got you setting is Auto (Hard bots only), On or Off (WG1, HO1, TL1)
- Skipping the round: once out, hold Jump for half a second to play the rest at six times speed with the HUD hidden; the hint names Jump's bound key, and the pause menu's Skip to next round shows while you can skip (WG1)
- Round result and table: a banner says WON, LOST or DRAW from your side at each round's end, then the round's table slides in with each team's score, every player's hits, BBs fired, time in play and who hit them; click for the next round (RR1)
- Scoreboard on Tab: hold Tab for the match table over the field, with each team's score, each player's stats and Pro tips against Hard bots (SB1, TL1)
- Depot: an indoor airsoft hall, 108 x 72 m under a roof with trusses and lamps, 5v5 or 6v6: three bent lanes round plywood rooms and racking, nine links with two dark tunnels, not mirrored but timed even, bots seeing 55 m; a BB fired up stops at the steel ceiling; positional sound and an ambience loop; BBs puff on metal, wood or concrete in its colour (FP1, DR1)
- Doors on Depot: 15 green hinged doors, some starting each round open and some shut; U (rebindable) opens or shuts the one in reach, with a prompt under the crosshair; a shut door stops BBs, bombs, sight and bodies; the creak and bang carry 20 m through walls and show on the other team's minimap; a door won't shut on someone in the doorway; bots and walk-offs open the doors in their way (DO1)
- Bots: Alpha with two bot teammates against three bots, Elimination rounds, first to five; bots see a 120 degree view, hear sound through walls muffled, take cover, peek, lean, hunt and flank, and vault low walls on their routes; the other team plays Easy, Normal or Hard (Hard pre-aims, slices corners and plays as a team) with kits from the bot kit table picked by difficulty, and your teammates play its level (BE1, BV1, BK1, DF1, DF2a, DF2b, TL1)
- Bot names and personalities: every bot has a name and one of four personalities (rusher, anchor, flanker, careful) that changes how it plays, never how well; names show in the Dev tab and while spectating (BN1)
- Bots by night: in the dark a bot sees you from 25 m in the open, 10 m under the trees and 15 m indoors, but from 40 m when you stand in lamplight or firelight; bots keep out of the light when they hold or hunt (WL6, TL1)
- Bots hold fire where a teammate could run into their line before the BBs arrive, a sprinting teammate round its route's corners included; a vault started against a wall's face carries on over it (WL10)
- Bot callouts: bots decide who calls what and when from `data/callouts.md` (contact, heard, down, last one, reloading, pushing, flanking, checking, holding, the flag), one call a team at a time with the browser's pace, on their own seeded stream so play never changes; your teammates' calls show in words in the hit feed with a radio click panned toward the speaker, an enemy keying up within 15 m is heard as a muffled click, and Callouts on Settings › HUD turns both off (CO1, CO2)
- Pings: the middle mouse button (rebindable, Squad: ping a spot) marks where the crosshair meets the field up to 120 m away, or an opponent you can see as a contact; one ping each at a time, a double click is one ping, cleared each round; bot teammates keep it in mind 20 s, one goes to look (or watches it when under your order or holding a post) and answers in the feed, a teammate in a fight carries on, and a teammate calling Contact may mark that enemy for you; nothing while out or on the range; your team's pings show on screen as a ring over the floor ("PING · 9 M") or a diamond where an enemy was seen ("CONTACT · 13 M"), pinned under the minimap when off screen and on the minimap, for 8 s, with a soft pip for your own; Settings › HUD › Ping markers hides the markers only (PN1, PN2)
- Magazine hand-off rules: one press asks the nearest teammate within 30 m with a spare that fits for a magazine when you are low (less than one full spare), or tosses one to a teammate asking you within 6 m in sight; an ask stands 15 s, a magazine flies 0.3 s plus 0.05 s a metre and lands in the catcher's emptiest spare, or is lost if they are out or the round is decided (MH1)
- Bots share magazines: a bot low on its rifle's BBs asks the nearest teammate with one to spare ("Need a mag!"); the one asked calls "Mag coming!", sprints over, walks the last metres and tosses it once in reach and sight, never giving away its own last full spare but handing over an unused pistol's freely; fights and cover come first, then bringing one, then squad orders; a catch gets a "Thanks for the mag!" now and then; Dev › Bots run low on BBs puts one bot a team on its last magazine 8 s into each round (MH2)
- Need a Mag: H or the order wheel's Need a Mag asks a teammate for a magazine, and reads Toss a Mag while a teammate asking you is in reach; the line under the crosshair says who needs one and how far, whose is coming and what you caught, a magazine glyph marks the chevron of whoever is asking you or bringing you one, and the magazine arcs and spins from hand to chest; Settings › HUD › Mag hand-off hides the line and glyphs (MH3)
- Full-screen map: hold M for the whole field, north up, showing what the minimap shows and the area names; while it's open the mouse moves a pointer, a click sends your squad there and the ping key pings the spot under it (FM1)
- Go there: a squad order on Mouse 4, a slice on the order wheel (six items now) or a click on the full-screen map; bots walk side by side to the spot and hold there looking the way they went, and with your view they stop at the first thing you look at (a wall, its foot on your side); the marker reads "Go · 30 m" on the way (FM1)
- Weapon torch (night only): a Light slot in Customise fits a torch to any replica; T (rebindable) switches it with a click; it goes out when you holster, are hit or a round starts; your own is a real light from Medium detail, everyone else's a visible beam, and a lit torch lets bots see its holder from 40 m; bots carry one at night and switch it by what they are doing (WL7)
- Glowing BBs: each replica in Customise picks At Night (the default), Always or Off; a glowing BB flies the same but is drawn green with a short green streak and never shrinks to nothing at range; bots load At Night (WL8)
- Bots play Attack / Defend: attackers walk to the middle then raise the flag, one on the rope and the others guarding it from cover; defenders hold near the pole, chase only what is near it, and the two nearest pull a raised flag down; two defenders on a lane stand side by side, and Hard pairs set a crossfire (FG2b, FG2, TL1)
- Levels and XP: matches give XP for playing, winning, each round you won and took part in, and each hit, less for a shorter match and multiplied by difficulty; levels 1 to 10 unlock replicas, power sources and parts; records keep wins and losses per difficulty and mode, best accuracy from 30 BBs and the longest win streak (LV1, SM1)
- Match numbers: every player's hits, times hit, friendly hits, BBs fired and time in play for the round and the match, and what got you on each hit (where from, how far, held angle, time in view), which the What got you card shows when your hit is called (MI1, WG1)
- Smoke and flash bombs: one smoke and one flash each round, thrown with G and Y (rebindable); smoke hides you from bots but never BBs, and a flash blinds anyone facing it up to 22 m, teammates included, and looking away from a flash keeps you safe; bots throw theirs too (SF1, SF2)

## Menus and settings

- Settings: a screen from the title screen and the pause menu for controls, audio, display and the FPS counter; every change applies and saves at once (SE1)
- HUD tab in Settings, after Display: crosshair shape, colour, size, thickness, gap, opacity, spread and outline with a live preview; hit marker; ammo counter; hit feed corner and fade; HUD panel opacity; What got you; Callouts (HO1, CO2)
- Dev tab: cheats and tools, always there with every switch off until it is turned on: game speed, ghost, bottomless magazines, spectate bots, retro pixels, reset save (DV2), disable levelling and reset level (LV1, FB3)
- Match screen: pick a map from picture cards (Depot or Woodland; Woodland's ground rolls and slopes under pine crowns, with grass, puddles and bushes that hide you and stop BBs, and fireflies by night), the mode (Elimination), your team (ALPHA or BETA) and a Bots section with two rows, Opponents and Teammates, each Easy, Normal or Hard (Teammates follow the opponents until picked), with a Rules section under the modes: Skirmish (rules as chips) or Custom (five switches: team size, from the picked map's own sizes: Depot and Woodland 5v5 or 6v6, rounds to win, round time, friendly fire, ricochets, each saved as you change it), and a Day / Night row for a map that offers both (none does now: Woodland plays at night, Depot by day, Settings › Dev › Time of day forces either); picks stay while the game is open (MN2, MN3, WL3, WL4, WL5)
- Practice on any field: pick Practice on the Match screen for no bots and no score, start at Alpha's first spawn with targets worked out from the map (lane points, the other team's spawns, along the lanes, steel, movers and swingers) and a timed challenge whose START stands ahead of you and lights only targets in sight; each map keeps its own best. The practice range stands in Depot's site with Depot's skyline, its own layout unchanged (WL9)
- Loading screen: PLAY shows the map's picture, name and a progress bar until the match draws (MN2)
- Pause menu: PAUSED, the map, mode and round, RESUME, SETTINGS, Skip to next round while you can skip, QUIT MATCH and the match's seed (MN2, WG1)
- Title screen and menus: a title with START, which opens a top bar of Match, Loadout, Armory, Settings and your level chip (MN1)
- Loadout and Customise screens: the LOADOUT tab with five saved loadouts, your carried replicas and a Performance sheet; CUSTOMISE sets each replica's colour, BB weight, hop-up and power source (LO2)
- Locked items: a replica, power source or part above your level is listed greyed with "LEVEL n" and can't be picked; the top bar's chip shows your level (LV1)
- Armory and end-of-match XP: the ARMORY tab with your level, XP, unlocks and levels, and the XP and level-up card in the middle of the match summary (AR1, SM1)
- Match summary: after the last round's table, a WON or LOST stamp with the score, map and bots; everyone's match numbers, the XP card, your records with NEW RECORD tags, What got you for your latest five hits and the next Pro tip; the next match starts after 15 s or on a click (SM1)
