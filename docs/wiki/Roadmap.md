# Roadmap

What is being built now and what comes after. **These are plans, not promises:** the order can change as the game is
played and tested. What has already arrived is in the [Patch Notes](Patch-Notes).

Airsoft is single player against bots, and stays that way: there is no multiplayer planned, ever.

## The road to 0.1

The game is built in **Dev** builds (0.1 Dev 1, Dev 2 and on), then **Beta** builds for balance, fixes and polish,
then **0.1**, which opens **Early Access**. Each build is posted on the
[Releases page](https://github.com/ggbillyjoe3526/airsoft/releases) with its patch notes. <!-- wiki:latest -->

### Built

Depot as an indoor airsoft hall and Woodland as an open wood (by day, or at night with the weapon torch and glowing
BBs), with doors and two flags; the AEG Rifle, the GBB Pistol, the Cyber Pistol and the Sniper Rifle with the 2x and
Variable Scopes, every part and power source, the Loadout, levels and the Armory; Easy, Normal and Hard bots with
names, personalities, kits and callouts, playing as a team; Elimination with its overtime flag, Attack / Defend and
Custom matches with 5 to 8 a side; the practice range in Depot's site, Practice on any field and the timed challenge;
squad orders, pings, Go there and the magazine hand-off; the minimap, the full-screen map, the hit feed, scoreboard
and match summary; smoke and flash bombs, thrown by you and the bots; detailed models of every replica, attachment,
magazine and bomb, your own arms holding them, and figures in real gear with faces; recorded sounds; graphics presets,
accessibility options, the save file screen, the crash screen and bug reports.
The rest of this wiki describes all of it.

### Next

- **0.1 Dev 3:** finished models for Depot and the practice range, and perhaps Woodland, a new replica: a compact
  electric SMG, quick on your feet and fast on the trigger, short on reach, and a new map: Kingsmere, a closed metro
  station on three levels, from the ticket hall down escalators, stairs and working lifts to the platforms, the
  trains and the tunnels (both in development).

### Then: the rest of the art pass

The replicas, attachments, figures and first-person arms have their finished models. The fields' greybox goes next:
finished models for Depot and everything in it (the range too), then the other fields, all made for the game and
checked in play under its own lighting.

## Early Access: the themed updates

After 0.1 the game grows through themed updates, each a small expansion built like 0.1, in Dev and Beta builds. The
order is still open: whichever comes first takes the next number.

### Replica Update

- A **holographic sight**.
- **More grenades** for the Loadout's Grenades slot, airsoft style: a CO2 sound grenade, and more from the smoke and
  flash bombs.
- **Chest rigs, belts and pouches** that decide what you carry, and an inventory.
- **Gas cooling:** rapid fire cools a gas replica's magazine and weakens its shots for a while.
- A chrono check before each match, more parts, and more replica types (maybe DMRs, SMGs and
  shotguns; later gas blowback rifles and LMGs).

### Modes Update

- **Extraction**, solo against bots that hunt you down on the way out.
- **Medic:** a hit player goes down and calls for a medic before a bleed-out timer runs out.
- **Field rule presets** like real sites: CQB, Speedsoft and Milsim, and house rules such as pistols only.
- **Survival** (hold a building against growing waves) and **Rush** (Attack / Defend in stages), and maybe Team
  Deathmatch, Domination or Capture the Flag.

### Maps Update

- **New fields**, built for airsoft: an abandoned hospital, a trench line with forts, a quarry and more.
- **Weather**: rain that masks footsteps, fog that shortens how far you see.
- **Prone**, with a field built for it.

### Progression Update

- **Challenges and badges** that pay XP.
- **A higher level cap**, with new replicas, parts and skins as rewards.
- **Replica skins and kit looks**: camouflage, plate carriers, helmets, goggles and patches. Looks only, never an
  advantage.

## 1.0

When the game is complete, Early Access ends and Airsoft is ready for everyone.
