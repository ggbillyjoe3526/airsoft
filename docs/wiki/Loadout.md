# Loadout

Open **Loadout** from the side rail. What you set here is what you carry into every match: your replicas, their parts,
power, BB weight, hop-up and colours. Every edit applies at once; there is no Save button.

## Saved loadouts

The **LOADOUT** tab keeps up to **five** named loadouts. Press **1** to **5** to pick one, and make a new one, rename,
duplicate, replace or remove it. The one picked is carried into the next match.

Each loadout has a **primary** and a **secondary** replica. The primary's card is the big one at the top, the
secondary's sits under it beside your grenades, and every replica shows at its true size against the others. Click a
card to pick that slot (or press its key), then pick a replica in the **Swap** row under them to carry it there;
replicas you haven't unlocked yet show greyed out with their level. A new player starts with the AEG Rifle and the GBB
Pistol. Every loadout carries the same **grenades** each round: one smoke bomb and one flash bomb
([Grenades](Grenades)). Picking what you carry comes with chest rigs and pouches in a later update
([Roadmap](Roadmap)).

## The Performance sheet

Beside your replicas, the Performance sheet compares your setup with the replica as it comes: **better in green,
worse in red**. It covers energy, fire rate, magazines, handling and the rest, adds **Zoom aimed** and **Aim in** for
an optic and a **Rattles** warning for a hi-cap magazine, and says when the [site limit](Replicas#site-limits) capped
the energy. Point at the panel to read what got better and worse in words.

## Customise

**CUSTOMISE**, on the picked replica's card, tunes one replica at a time:

- **Colour:** eight bold schemes, four plain ones, and a **Realistic colours everywhere** switch that shows every bold
  scheme as its plain colour, yours and the bots'.
- **BB weight**, from 0.20 g to 0.30 g, with a picture of the BB's flight, and the **hop-up** dial's starting
  setting. **Glowing BBs** under it: At Night (the default), Always or Off. A glowing BB is drawn green, a little
  larger at range, with a longer streak, so you can follow a shot all the way; it flies exactly like a white one.
- **Power source:** which battery or gas it runs on ([Parts and Power](Parts-and-Power#power-sources)).
- **Part tabs:** optic, grip, laser, barrel, muzzle part, magazine and light ([weapon torch](Parts-and-Power#lights)). A tab the replica can't take shows greyed out,
  with the mount it needs. Each part's card shows its picture and what it changes, better in green and worse in red.

The replica stands on a turntable at the top, with the parts fitted and in your colours. It stays the same size and in
the same place whatever you fit, so you can see a part change it. Drag it (either mouse button) to turn it, scroll to
zoom, or pick a view: **3/4**, **Left**, **Right** or **Top**. **R** puts it back to 3/4.

On the Loadout page itself every replica is drawn to the same scale as the others, so a pistol looks small beside a
rifle.

## From the practice range

On the [Practice Range](Practice-Range), press **L** (or pick Loadout in the pause menu) to open the Loadout on the
replica in your hand. Change what you like, then press L again, Esc or **BACK TO PRACTICE** to go straight back to
where you stood.

## Bots' replicas

Your bot teammates carry the replicas as they come. The other team's bots carry kits picked by difficulty
([Bots](Bots#kits)).
