# Controls

Airsoft plays with a mouse and keyboard. The mouse is captured while you play; Esc frees it and pauses.

## Default keys

Every action can be changed in **Settings › Controls**, with up to two keys each. Keys are named by where they sit,
so W A S D stays W A S D on any keyboard layout.

<!-- wiki:controls -->

- **Crouch** and **Aim** are held by default: let go to stand up or lower the replica. Either can be set to toggle
  in Settings › Controls; a toggled crouch stands you up when you sprint or jump.
- **Lean** and **Sprint** are always held.
- **Jump** vaults when you are at a low wall: "Space to vault" shows under the crosshair (it names your own Jump
  key).
- **Loadout (practice)** opens the Loadout from the [Practice Range](Practice-Range); in a match it does nothing.
- **Hop-up less** and **Hop-up more** turn the hop-up dial on the replica in your hands ([How to Play](How-to-Play#hop-up)).
- **Squad: ping a spot** (the middle mouse button) marks where you look for your bot teammates
  ([Squad Orders](Squad-Orders#pings)).
- **Squad: need a mag** (H) tosses a magazine to a teammate who asked you, or asks for one when you are low
  ([Squad Orders](Squad-Orders#need-a-mag)); it is also the order wheel's fifth item.
- **Throw smoke** (4) and **Throw flash** (5) throw a smoke bomb or a flash bomb where you look at once
  ([Grenades](Grenades)).
- **Next / previous replica** (the mouse wheel) steps through your two replicas and then your bombs left, smoke then
  flash, and round again. It never stops on the holster. With a bomb in hand, a trigger pull throws it.
- **Open / shut door** (G) opens or shuts the door within reach, the one nearest where you look. A line under the
  crosshair says which while you stand by one ([Maps](Maps#doors)).
- **Fire** also skips the rest of a round once you are out ([How to Play](How-to-Play#skipping-the-rest-of-the-round)).
- A save from before these keys moved keeps your own changes: an action still on its old default key (smoke on G, flash
  on Y, doors on U, the hop-up dial on 4 and 5 as well as Page Down and Page Up) takes its new one.

## Keys you can't change

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Esc** | Pause in a match. In the menus it closes what is open first (a dialog, a key waiting to be rebound, a rename), then asks **Quit game?** |
| **F11** or **Alt+Enter** | Switch between windowed and fullscreen |
| **F3** | Performance readout: frame rate, frame time, the match seed and more |
| **F4** | Show every BB's flight path in the world |
| **F5** | Show the hit areas on every character, through walls |

F4 and F5 are views for checking how the game works; they are off by default and cost nothing while off.

## Rebinding

- Pick an action in **Settings › Controls** and press the new key or mouse button.
- Taking a key another action already uses swaps the two, so nothing is left without a key by accident.
- Fire and the four move keys can never be left without a key.
- Mouse sensitivity can be set for the hip and for aiming, also as centimetres per 360° turn at your mouse's DPI.
  You can invert vertical look too.
