# Grenades

Every loadout has <!-- wiki:config grenade_config.slots --> **grenade slots**, and you pick a **smoke bomb** or a **flash bomb** for each on
the [Loadout](Loadout) screen: one of each, two smokes or two flashes. A new loadout carries one of each. Bots carry
what their kit lists. Your bombs come back at the start of every round, and on the [Practice Range](Practice-Range)
after every throw. The replica panel at the bottom right shows how many you have left.

## Throwing

Press **Throw smoke** (4) or **Throw flash** (5) ([Controls](Controls)), or roll the mouse wheel past your replicas,
to take a bomb in hand: your replica goes down, your gloved hand comes up holding the bomb in a fist, the panel says
**SMOKE IN HAND** or **FLASH IN HAND** and the crosshair stays to throw by. Nothing is thrown until you click: a click
throws it, or hold the button and your arm stays cocked back, the pin out, until you let go.
Rolling on, or a replica key, reload or the holster, puts it back. Your replica goes down while your left hand pulls
the pin and your arm swings the bomb over, it leaves your hand (a little right of and ahead of your eyes, never from
inside a wall you stand against) along your view, lofted a little, and your replica comes back up: under a second in
all. A thrown bomb flies and lands without its pin. You
can't fire or aim while you throw, and a reload under way stops. If you keep the trigger held after the pull that
threw the bomb, your replica doesn't fire when it comes back up: let go and pull again. Throwing on the run adds some
of your own speed.

Bots throw the same way: you see the bomb in a bot's hand as its arm swings back by its head, then over and out as it
lets go.

Bombs bounce off walls, the ground and anyone in the game standing in the way (it doesn't hit them), then roll and
settle. Just after the throw a bomb passes through the thrower's teammates, so one thrown past a teammate at your side
never bounces back at you. If you are hit while you pull the pin, you keep the bomb.

You can hear a bomb coming: the ring pull as the pin comes out, then a tin can clattering where it lands. A smoke bomb
hisses loudly as it goes off; a flash bomb goes off with a loud, sharp pop that echoes round the field as it dies
away.

## Smoke bomb

A grey canister in glossy printed film with a green stripe and a stencilled label, a matte black lever down its side
and a pull ring on top.

A smoke bomb pops <!-- wiki:config grenade_config.smoke_fuse --> seconds after it leaves your hand and smokes where it
lies for <!-- wiki:config grenade_config.smoke_time --> seconds: a white cloud about <!-- wiki:config grenade_config.smoke_radius --> metres out from the canister each way, thinning away at the end.

- **Nobody sees through it**, bots included. Up close inside the cloud you can still make out someone right beside
  you.
- **BBs fly straight through it.** Firing blind into smoke can still hit.
- Standing inside a cloud fills your screen with smoke.

Use it to cross open ground, to cut off a long angle, or to cover a retreat.

## Flash bomb

A slim olive card tube with a violet band round its top, a printed label and a pull ring hanging from the rim.

A flash bomb goes off <!-- wiki:config grenade_config.flash_fuse --> seconds after it leaves your hand with a bright
flash and a bang. Anyone with a clear line from their eyes to it is **dazzled**: your screen goes white and the sound
is dulled, and a dazzled bot can't see anything and ducks where it stands.

- How long a dazzle lasts depends on how close you are and how directly you were looking at it, from <!-- wiki:config grenade_config.dazzle_min --> seconds at
  the edge to <!-- wiki:config grenade_config.dazzle_max --> seconds looking straight at it up close. **Turn away**: side
  on it still gets you, but looking away from it, nothing does.
- A wall between you and the flash keeps you safe. Smoke doesn't.
- **It dazzles everyone,** your own team and you too. Throw it round a corner, not at your own feet.

## Bots

Bots carry two bombs each, as their kit lists (some carry two smokes or two flashes), throw them, and react to yours: smoke hides you from them, and a flash blinds them for a
moment.

- **A bot pinned in cover** by someone shooting at it from range may smoke the line between them.
- **A bot pushing round a corner** to where it heard you may flash it first, then look away until the bang and come
  through. It never flashes where a teammate close by would see it, counting teammates heading that way, and it
  checks again as it throws.
- **A Normal or Hard bot that sees your flash coming** may turn away from it until the bang, and isn't blinded
  (Hard more often; an Easy bot never does). A flash that lands out of its sight is surest.
- **Bots walk round a standing smoke cloud** where the way round is open, yours or their own, and through it only
  where there is no other way: a cloud in the only doorway does not stop them.
- Bots hear a bang or a smoke's pop and come to look. Harder bots throw more often.
