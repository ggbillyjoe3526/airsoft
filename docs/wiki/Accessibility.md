# Accessibility

**Settings › Accessibility** holds the options below. Each applies at once, even from the pause menu, and the tab
shows a live sample. [Settings](Settings#hud) also has HUD size, crosshair and hit marker options, and every key can
be rebound ([Controls](Controls)).

## Team colours

**Standard** (blue and orange) or **High contrast**, a light blue and a dark orange checked for colour blindness.
High contrast covers the figures, the flag and the whole HUD, and its figures glow faintly in daylight. A new set
shows from your next match; the Match screen and HUD previews change at once. No prop on a field is painted near a
team colour, so nothing in the scenery reads as a player.

## Gauge patterns

On by default. Beta's score pips, score block and flag gauge are **striped** while Alpha's stay solid, in both colour
sets, so the teams read apart without colour. A low magazine turns yellow **and** striped.

## Sound cues

Off by default. Shows the sounds you hear as markers on a ring round the crosshair:

- the other team's footsteps and vaults as two dots,
- shots as an arrowhead pointing out,
- hit calls as a **HIT** tag.

The ring turns with your view; nearer sounds are stronger, and a silenced shot is heard half as far. Your own sounds
and your teammates' steps show nothing. Markers fade after about a second and a half. Pick their colour and size.

## Reduced motion

**On**, **Off** or **Follow system** (your computer's reduce-motion setting). It takes out the replica's sway and the
drifting dust, halves the kick and the reload tilt, softens lean and vault tilt to a quarter, holds chimney smoke still
and holds the flag warning steady instead of pulsing. Recoil climb stays the same, so aiming feels the same. A hit
never moves the camera, with or without it.

## Screen reader messages

On by default. A screen reader such as NVDA on Windows or Orca on Linux reads out the match: hit calls ("Rook called
HIT · You"), your own hit ("You're hit · Rook"), each round's start and end with the score, and the match result. Your
hit and the result interrupt; the rest wait their turn. Your teammates' callouts are read too, each as it shows in the
feed ("Rook: Two, Box yard!"), waiting their turn. Your squad orders are read ("Order · Follow Me"), and so is an
order that has to wait for the next round. Skipping the rest of a round is read as it starts, and the round's end
after it. In the menus, Settings' choices, switches and sliders, Customise's paint tiles and the Armory's level track
have spoken names; the Match page's and the Loadout's cards don't yet.
