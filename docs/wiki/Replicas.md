# Replicas

Replicas are the toy guns of airsoft: plastic, light, and they fire 6 mm BBs. In Airsoft every replica is an
original design, and they differ in how they load, cycle, sound and handle, **never in damage**. One hit is one hit,
whatever it came from.

You carry two at once, a primary and a sidearm, picked in your [Loadout](Loadout). In play you see your own arms, in
dark olive gloves and sleeves, holding the one in your hands: the AEG Rifle and the Sniper Rifle in both hands, a pistol
in your right hand, held right of the middle of the screen with only your hand and wrist in view. With a grip fitted
your left hand holds the grip. On a reload your left hand takes the empty magazine out, carries it down out of view
and brings a fresh one up in the hand to push it home; the replica tips up toward you while it does, so you see your
hand at work and not your forearm.

## Stats

The numbers below are read from the game's own stats file, so they are always the numbers you play with. They are
each replica as it comes, before [parts and power sources](Parts-and-Power).

<!-- wiki:replicas -->

- **Muzzle energy** is how hard the replica pushes its BB, the way an airsoft site's chrono rates it. With the BB
  weight it sets the **muzzle speed**: more speed means a flatter, quicker shot that the wind pushes less.
- **Fire rate** is BBs a second with the trigger held (auto, or within a burst), or the fastest you can click (single
  shots).
- **Magazines carried** includes the loaded one. Nothing refills during a round.
- **Draw** is the time to bring the replica up after a switch.
- **Spread** is how far shots scatter from the hip, before stance and movement. Aiming takes it to half the
  replica's base spread, so the Sniper Rifle, wide from the hip, tightens the most.
- **Recoil** is each shot's upward kick, light, because these are toys.

## AEG Rifle

An electric rifle (an AEG, "automatic electric gun"), with a motor whirr on every shot. It fires **single shots,
3-round bursts or full auto**: press **B** to change mode, and the fire selector on the replica shows the mode you
are in. On an indoor field (Depot) it starts each round on single shots: [no full auto indoors](Maps#depot).
A battery drives it, so the battery you fit sets its rate of fire. It is a short carbine, about 67 cm long: a
quad-rail handguard with a ribbed cover, an A-frame front sight, a small ghost-ring rear sight, a raked grip moulded
in one piece with the receiver, an adjustable stock and a curved magazine. [Inspect](Controls) it (**Y**) to see the
charging handle pulled back, the dust cover open and the hop-up dial turn.

The AEG Rifle takes the most parts: an optic on its top rail, a grip under it, a laser on its side rail, a barrel, a
muzzle part and a different magazine. It is in your kit from the start.

## GBB Pistol

A gas blowback pistol: every shot cycles the slide with a puff of gas from the muzzle and the ejection port. It fires
**single shots** only. Its slide locks back when you fire the last BB and closes as the new magazine seats near the
end of the reload, so you always know when it is empty: you hear it lock back on that last shot. Only the slide moves:
with it back, the barrel shows in front of it.

Gas drives it, so the gas you load sets its energy: stronger gas pushes harder and kicks harder. It is a compact
striker pistol: a squared slide with rounded edges, a chamfered nose and serrations at the front and back, a
railed dust cover, a raked grip with stippled panels
and finger grooves, and a thumb rest. Its sights are a white U at the back and a post with a white dot at the front:
aimed, the post sits in the U. It takes a laser, a silencer and an extended magazine. It is in your kit from
the start.

## Cyber Pistol

A futuristic electric pistol, unlocked at level 15, the top of the [levels](Levels-and-Unlocks). It fires **single
shots, bursts or full auto**, faster than the rifle from big magazines, with almost no kick (the table above has
its numbers). Its battery is built in, so
no power source fits it, and only the Weapon Torch fits it.
It is the same size in your hand as the GBB Pistol. It wears its own white, cyan and magenta colours, with
glowing slashes, a pink core behind bars in its slide, a finned rear cap and three fire-mode lights; with
**Realistic colours** on it turns plain grey with no glow. It has its own shot and magazine sounds.

## Sniper Rifle

A spring-powered bolt-action rifle, unlocked at level 5. It fires **single shots** only: after every shot your right
hand leaves the grip, draws the bolt back over the spring and snaps it home, and is back on the grip as the next shot
is ready: the bolt and its sound finish just as the rifle can fire again, and from the hip the rifle tips toward you
while you work it, so your hand stays in view. It shoots harder and further than the AEG Rifle, from small magazines, and has its own [site limit](#site-limits).

It comes with the **2x Scope** fitted; take it off or swap it for another optic in the Loadout. It is made to be aimed:
from the hip its shots scatter widely, and the spread closes as the sight comes up. The spring you fit sets its energy
([Parts and Power](Parts-and-Power#power-sources)). It takes the AEG Rifle's optics, grips and muzzle parts, but no
barrels or other magazines. It is a long rifle in a grey chassis: a plain barrel with a thread cap, a slotted forend,
a black receiver with a top rail, a short box magazine, a vertical grip and a stock with a cheek piece and a rubber
butt pad.

## How they sound

Every replica's shots and magazines are made from real recordings: a gas pistol, an electric motor's whirr and hands
on magazines, recorded for the game. Pull the trigger with nothing loaded and every replica gives the same plastic
dry click. You can tell them apart by ear: the AEG Rifle's motor whirr, the GBB Pistol's gas pop and slide, and the
Cyber Pistol's short buzz. BBs sound different on metal, wood, glass, plastic,
cardboard and on a player, so you hear what a shot hit.

## BB weight

Every replica can load any BB weight from **0.20 g to 0.30 g**, picked per replica in the [Loadout](Loadout). A
heavier BB leaves the barrel a little slower but holds its line better in the wind; a lighter one is quicker but drifts
more. Each replica above lists its factory weight.

## Site limits

As at a real site, each class of replica has a most energy it may shoot with. Parts that add energy (stronger gas, a
long barrel) stop at the limit, and the Loadout's Performance sheet says when a limit capped your setup.

## Colours

Each replica can wear one of eight bold colour schemes or four plain ones, picked in **Customise**. Bots hold theirs
in their team's colours. **Realistic colours** shows every bold scheme as its plain colour, yours and the bots', from
the next match.

In your hands a replica is made of what the real thing would be, under whatever colours it wears: the GBB Pistol's
slide is brushed steel, the AEG Rifle's receiver and rail are anodised aluminium, the
barrels and the Sniper Rifle's action are steel, and the grips, stocks and the pistol's frame are matte polymer with a
moulded grain. The metal catches the light; the polymer does not.

The AEG Rifle and the GBB Pistol look lightly used in your hands: faint scratches, rubbed edges and a little dirt in
the recesses, in whatever colours they wear. Their swappable magazines (Hi-Cap, Low-Cap and Extended) look used the same way.

## On other players

Bots hold the same replicas you hold, with the parts they have fitted: their optic (the iron sights taken off under
it), silencer, grip, torch or laser, and the magazine they reload.
A rifle is held with its stock in the shoulder and the head down on it, looking along the sights, the other hand
under the handguard (just behind a fitted grip); a pistol is held out in one hand. Their hands hold each replica as
yours do in first person: high on the grip, index finger on the trigger, thumb round the grip's left side.
