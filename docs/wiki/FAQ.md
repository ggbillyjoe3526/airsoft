# FAQ

### Is there multiplayer?

No. Airsoft is single player against bots, and that won't change. Your teammates and opponents are all
[bots](Bots), and you can give your teammates [orders](Squad-Orders).

### Do I have to pay for anything in the game?

No. There are no microtransactions, loot boxes or paid advantages. Everyone starts with a kit that can win, and
everything else unlocks by playing ([Levels and Unlocks](Levels-and-Unlocks)).

### One BB took me out. Is that a bug?

No, that's airsoft: one hit is one hit, wherever it lands, for everyone. Replicas differ in how they shoot and handle,
never in damage ([How to Play](How-to-Play#one-hit-and-youre-out)).

### A BB hit me and I'm still in. Why?

It bounced first. By default a ricochet doesn't count, and you see "Ricochet: play on". A Custom match can make
ricochets count ([Modes](Modes#skirmish-and-custom)).

### Why do my shots drop at long range?

BBs are real projectiles: they slow, fall and drift with the wind. Turn the hop-up dial (Page Down and Page Up) to keep them flat
for longer, aim a little high past each replica's flat range, and try the [Practice Range](Practice-Range) readout.

### Is there blood?

No. BBs puff on what they hit, and a hit player raises a hand and walks off. Replicas are toys, not firearms.

### Are the replicas real models?

No. Every replica is an original design, with no real brand names or trademarked designs.

### Can I use a controller?

Not yet: Airsoft plays with a mouse and keyboard.

### Is there a Mac version?

Windows comes first and Linux is built with every release; macOS comes later.

### Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

The installer isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**
([Getting Started](Getting-Started#installing-on-windows)).

### The game won't start, or the screen stays black.

Use the **Airsoft (safe graphics)** shortcut, which starts the game on the simpler renderer. If the game closed while
drawing its graphics, it restarts on safe graphics by itself ([Settings](Settings#graphics)).

### Where is my save?

**Settings › Save file** shows the folder and has an **Open folder** button. The game also keeps three daily restore
points ([Saving and Bug Reports](Saving-and-Bug-Reports)).

### Why are there only two fields?

Depot and Woodland are the fields for now. New fields are on the [Roadmap](Roadmap).

### How do I report a bug?

**Report a Bug** on the title screen or in the pause menu fills in a GitHub issue for you; nothing is sent until you
press Submit ([Saving and Bug Reports](Saving-and-Bug-Reports#report-a-bug)).

### Is this wiki up to date?

Yes. It is rebuilt from the game's own files every time the game changes: the stats, unlocks, maps, keys and patch
notes are read straight from them, and every update that changes what you see in play updates these pages too.
