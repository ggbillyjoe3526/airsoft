# Levels and Unlocks

Everyone starts with a full kit that can win any match. Playing earns **XP**, XP raises your **level**, and each
level unlocks more replicas, power sources and parts to play with. Unlocks are kept for good, and nothing is for sale:
there are no microtransactions, loot boxes or paid advantages.

## What each level unlocks

<!-- wiki:unlocks -->

Locked items stay listed in the [Loadout](Loadout), greyed out with **LEVEL n**, so you can see what's coming. The
top bar shows your level. At the top level, XP stops counting until more levels come in a later update.

## Earning XP

A finished match pays:

<!-- wiki:xp -->

- **The bots' level multiplies the total:** the lower of your opponents' and your teammates' levels (the opponents'
  alone when you have no teammates). A [Custom](Modes#skirmish-and-custom) match pays at most the Custom multiplier.
  The card after the match names the one that counted: "Hard bots", "Easy teammates" or "Custom rules".
- **A round won counts only if you took part in it:** you hit an opponent in it, or were still in when it ended.
- **A shorter Custom match pays less:** Match played, Match won and each Round won are scaled by its rounds to win
  divided by 5 (never more than in full).
- A first-to-5 match on Normal gives about 250 XP, so the first level comes after your first match and the top level
  after about 25 matches on Normal (fewer on Hard).
- The [Practice Range](Practice-Range) gives no XP, and nor does a match played with a Dev setting that changes how it
  plays.

After the last round, the match summary lists the match's XP line by line, and a level-up card names what you
unlocked. A match that gave no XP says why.

## Records

The game keeps your **wins and losses** for each bot level and mode, your **best accuracy** (counted from 30 BBs
fired in a match) and your **longest win streak**. A new record gets a **NEW RECORD** tag on the match summary.

A match counts for your records when your teammates play at the opponents' level, or at the level they follow by
default. Custom matches never count, and the summary says "Not counted this match" when one doesn't.

## The Armory

The **ARMORY** tab on the top bar shows your level, the XP bar and what the next level takes, the XP you have earned
in all, how many items are unlocked, the next unlock with its picture, and every level's items marked unlocked, next
or locked. It also shows how XP is earned and the bot level multipliers.

Your level, unlocks and records are saved in their own file, apart from your settings, so a crash never loses them
([Saving and Bug Reports](Saving-and-Bug-Reports)).
