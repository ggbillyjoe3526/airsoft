# Movement

Movement in Airsoft is grounded: no wall-running, no slides, a jump that clears a low step and little more. Where you
stand and how quietly you get there win more fights than fast hands.

## Paces

<!-- wiki:movement -->

- **Run** is how you move when you press nothing else.
- **Walk** (hold Shift) is slow and silent, and almost as steady to shoot from as standing still.
- **Crouch** (hold C or Ctrl) makes you a small target and hides you behind waist-high cover. Moving crouched is
  silent too, so you can sneak up on a bot that hasn't seen you. Crouched you fit through low tunnels (Woodland's are
  1.4 m high); under one you stay down until you're out, whatever you hold, and bots crouch through them too.
- **Sprint** (hold Alt) is fastest and loudest. You can still fire, but your shots spread wide; your replica comes up
  out of the carry, pointing where you aim, while you fire.
- **Holstered** (press 3) you run a little faster than with your replica out, never faster than a sprint, and your
  sprint is a little faster too.

Every figure's legs keep pace with how fast it moves: its feet plant on the ground without sliding, a run kicks the
heels up, a sprint leans in, and a step aside turns the legs into it while the replica stays on its aim. What you see
of a running player's legs is what a BB has to hit.

Running, sprinting, vaulting and landing all make noise that players and bots hear. Running or landing on gravel or
steel carries further, and so does running into a noisy prop such as a container, a steel drum or a plastic barrel ([Maps](Maps#depot)).

Pushing through **bushes and ferns** slows you to three quarters of your speed, whatever your pace, and running or
sprinting through them rustles the leaves loudly enough to be heard further than on open ground. Walk or crouch through
quietly ([Maps](Maps#woodland)). Bots are slowed and heard the same way.

Wading through Woodland's **stream** slows you to 60% of your pace, and every stride splashes, at any pace: walking or
crouching softens it, but it still carries nearly as far as a run on dry ground ([Maps](Maps#woodland)). Bots are slowed and heard the same way.

## Lean

Hold **Q** or **E** to lean left or right round a corner. You slow to a walk while leaning. A lean that a wall cuts
short eases back instead of poking you through it. You can be hit only where you appear: your leaning head and
shoulder show exactly as far as a BB could hit them.

## Vault

Press **Jump** at a low wall to vault over it; "Space to vault" shows under the crosshair (it names your own Jump key)
whenever a press would vault. A vault takes under half a second, carries your replica with you, and you can't fire
for a moment after it. Vaults are loud, and you can't vault while crouched. Waist-high crouch cover and the plywood
port walls on [Depot](Maps#depot) can't be vaulted.

Bots vault the same low walls the same way, when it saves them a long walk round.

## Climb

Press **Jump** at a ledge of the same height whose flat top runs on, such as a station platform's edge, to climb up
onto it: "Space to climb" shows under the crosshair. You put a hand on the
edge, pull up and stand on the top in about half a second, your replica held low until you are up. Crates and other
props are never climbed, only vaulted. Bots climb up and drop back down the same way.

Bots running up stairs lean into the climb with their knees high, and going down they sit back a little on softer
steps.

## Holster

Press **3** to holster your replica: it drops out of view, the panel says **HOLSTERED** and the crosshair goes.
A fresh trigger pull, aim, reload or replica key (1, 2 or the mouse wheel) draws it again. Drawing from the holster
takes longer than a normal switch, so holster for a long run, not in a fight. Bots holster too, on long calm runs in
their own half, and draw as soon as there's a threat. Sprinting with it away, a bot runs with its elbows bent and its
hands low and close in, swinging easily opposite its legs.

Other players and bots wear whatever isn't in their hands: a pistol sits in the holster on the right hip and a rifle
rests on the backpack. That's true while holstered, and also for the replica they aren't using.

## Switching replicas

You carry two replicas, set in your [Loadout](Loadout). Press **1** or **2**, or roll the mouse wheel, to switch. The
wheel carries on past them onto your bombs ([Grenades](Grenades#throwing)), never onto the holster.
Each replica has its own draw time ([Replicas](Replicas)).
