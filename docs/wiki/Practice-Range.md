# Practice Range

The practice range is where you learn how BBs fly: no bots, no score and no XP. It stands in its own lot beside
[Depot](Maps#depot)'s yard, under the same sun and sky. Pick **Range** on the Match screen (its only mode is Practice)
and press **PRACTICE**. You can also practise on a field: see [Practice on a field](#practice-on-a-field).

![The range in Depot's site: three lanes of targets, with Depot's water tower and buildings beyond the walls](images/range-in-depot.jpg)

## The targets

Three lanes of targets run out from the firing line. Beyond the walls stand Depot's buildings, its water tower,
cranes, light masts, watch tower and smoking chimneys.

<!-- wiki:range-targets -->

There is no hit marker on the range: steel plates ring and fall, and figures fall, so you see every hit where it
lands. The **START** plate starts the timed challenge.

## The readout

After each shot a readout names your last BB's target and distance, where it landed, or that it flew out, with a tip
such as "Aim lower, or turn the hop-up down". Use it with the hop-up dial (Page Down and Page Up) to learn how far each replica stays
flat ([How to Play](How-to-Play#hop-up)).

## Timed challenge

Press **Esc** for the range's pause menu and switch **Timed challenge** on; the game remembers it between launches.

1. Shoot the **START** plate. The range counts 3-2-1, **GO!**
2. A bar shows your time and hits while you clear the targets.
3. **TIME!** shows your hits, shots and misses, and **NEW BEST** when you beat your best time.

Each map keeps its own best, so the range's and Depot's are separate. A run with a Dev setting on says **NOT KEPT**
instead.

## Practice on a field

Every field offers **Practice** too: pick the field, then Practice, and press **PRACTICE**. There are no bots and
no score, and you start at one end of the field.

- **Targets stand where the other team would play:** steel plates, swinging plates, figures (crouched behind low
  cover where there is some), pop-ups and moving figures, each facing back towards you.
- **The START plate** stands a few steps ahead of where you start. In the timed challenge a target lights only while
  you can see it, and it stays lit longer the further it is from you.
- **The readout** says how far from you each BB landed, and a BB is only lost when it falls off the map.
- **The minimap** shows, captioned Practice.
- **Woodland's practice is by day**, its first time of day.
- Practice stays picked when you pick another field. After the range, the next field gives you back the mode you had
  before.

## Changing your loadout

Press **L** (or pick Loadout in the pause menu) to open the [Loadout](Loadout) on the replica in your hand, with a
FROM THE RANGE note. Swap or tune your replicas, then press L again, Esc or **BACK TO PRACTICE**: you are back where
you stood, facing the same way, crouched or holstered as before, with your new setup. A timed run in progress is
dropped.

It works the same in practice on a field. Leaving practice takes you back to the Match screen.
