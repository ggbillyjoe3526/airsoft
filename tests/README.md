# Tests

Nothing under `tests/` ships; every export preset leaves it out.

| Folder | What | Run by |
|---|---|---|
| `unit/` | GUT tests, `test_*.gd`, one file per system | `tools/unit_tests.sh`, in `tools/check.sh` and CI |
| `guards/` | Whole bot matches over 16 seeds a field, checked against the browser game's limits (`docs/records/MG1.md`): Depot by day and Woodland at 5v5 at night (`test_woodland_guards.gd`, WL10) | `tools/match_guards.sh`, `tools/check.sh --guards` and CI's own "Match guards" jobs (five for the night guards) |
| `balance/` | The bot balance report: `BalanceReport` judges each figure against its band and standard error, `balance_run.gd` plays the matches (`docs/records/BR1.md`) | `tools/balance.sh`, by hand; never the checks or CI |
| `support/` | Helpers the tests share: `MatchRunner` plays a whole Elimination match headless, bots on both teams, and tallies it (the guards and the balance report read it; the route searches a tick and the longest wait for a route, WL2; a map that offers no day, or a runner given `night` (WR1), plays at night, its bots seeing by its NightField, WL6, and every character carrying the pool's torch there, `lights`, WL7; `sized_for` gives a map's team size, Woodland 5v5, and the height guards take the terrain, WL10); `MatchGuard` plays a field's guard matches at a level, by day or at night, and checks each (every file in `guards/` reads it; WL10); `FakeScreens` stands in for the window's DisplayServer calls (screens, mode, V-Sync); `HitAreaProbe` measures Hitbox's volume against the drawn figure (FI7); `FieldChecks` holds the rules every field keeps and its layout checks, `FieldStandard` the browser's numbers they're held to, with each field's exceptions, known failures and refused files (FC1), and `FieldMeasure` measures a field's sightlines and walks on its level's ray and nav grid (FC2); `OldWoodland` is the wood before WR1 (`support/maps/old_woodland.json`), for the tests written against its layout; `TowerBlock` is a small test field in storeys that offers both Day and Night (`support/maps/tower_block.json`), for what no shipped map has since Neon Heights went, and `DevContent` the shipped map catalogue or pool with one map or item tagged dev, now that every shipped map and item is public; `unit_pre_run.gd`, GUT's pre-run hook (`.gutconfig.json`), turns off headless Godot's 6.9 ms sleep after every frame (CI1) | – |
