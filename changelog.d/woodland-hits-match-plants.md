### Woodland trees and thickets stop shots where you see them
- On Woodland, shots and Spotted taps no longer stop on empty air beside the spruce thickets and trees by the stream's east bank.
- Each spruce in a thicket stops BBs and sight round its own shape, so a shot through the gap between two tops flies on.
- Tree trunks stop BBs at the bark, thick at the foot and thinner as they rise.
- The west camp's front palisade is taller, so its spawns stay hidden from the high ground.
