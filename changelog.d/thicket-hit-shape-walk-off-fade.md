### Thickets and walk-offs
- Woodland's spruce thickets now stop BBs, sight and Spotted where the drawn trees are: a shot or a Spotted tap past the boughs reaches the body behind.
- Hit players now always fade off the field before they come back, so a respawn no longer makes them blink out mid walk-off; while someone can see them they walk on first (up to 3 s more) and fade out of sight where they can.
- With the respawn wait set to zero, a hit player still waits for the hit call and the fade before coming back.
- On Subway, hit players at the east end now step down from the platform and walk off to the dead zone, instead of stopping and fading there.
