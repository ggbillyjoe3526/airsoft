### Subway station entrance
- The ticket hall has a station entrance right behind the street team's start.
- A doorway in the west wall shows a stair and an escalator up to a daylit street, with shop fronts, a railing and a lamp post.
- The station name board and a "Way out" sign hang over the doorway, and printed posters line the stair.
- A locked folding gate shuts the entrance. You can look, but nobody walks or shoots through it.

### Subway train rails
- The grab rails on the trains are now smooth, minimal bent tubes.
- Each seat bay has one rail and two poles standing on the floor, with no brackets.

### Brighter subway orange
- The subway's orange is brighter and keeps its full colour.
- This covers the rails, the trains' stripe, the seats' rings and the station's orange name boards.

### Subway train adverts
- The panels above the train windows show twelve made-up adverts.
- Each car shows the adverts in a different order.
