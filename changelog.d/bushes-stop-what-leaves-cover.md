### Bushes stop only what their leaves cover
- Ferns and shrubs on Woodland stop BBs and hide people only where their leaves are, not across their whole drawn size.
- A lone fern no longer hides a crouched player in its middle; a bed of ferns still does.
- A bot you can plainly see in a fern can now be hit and spotted.
- Spotted (Z) works on every map and mode, and teammates within 30 m are told where the spotted enemy is.
- The dark boxes at the foot of the spruce thickets are gone.
