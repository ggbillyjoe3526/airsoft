### GBB Pistol shoots harder
- The GBB Pistol now fires at 0.70 J, up from 0.52 J (274 fps with 0.20 g BBs, up from 237), like a real green-gas pistol.
- It stays on target to about 30 m, up from 27 m, and its BBs get there sooner. Red Gas takes it to about 32 m and Black Gas to about 33 m.
- Its hop-up now comes set at 50 %, not 55 %, so the stronger gas doesn't over-hop.
- It is still a sidearm: single shots, a hard kick, a wide spread and 18 BBs a magazine.

### Compact SMG shoots harder (Dev content)
- The Compact SMG, in development under Settings › Dev › Dev content, now fires at 0.85 J, up from 0.75 J (302 fps, up from 284).
- Its BBs get there sooner, and it stays on target to about 30 m, up from 27 m. Its best reach stays about 31 m, against the AEG Rifle's 39 m.
- If you set the Compact SMG's hop-up yourself, set it again in the Loadout: its dial's range changed, so an old setting now lifts a little less.
