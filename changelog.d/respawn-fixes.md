### Respawn fixes and loadout menu
- A round your team wins while you wait to come back now counts for you: your XP and the scoreboard's rounds won.
- Coming back, you face the way your spawn faces, with toggled aim and crouch let go.
- The "What got you" card and the hit lines from your hit no longer show when you come back.
- While you're out, the view stays on the teammate you were watching when another one comes back.
- The round table shows "Still in" for a player who was hit and came back, and dims only those still out.
- Bots no longer call "Last one" or "Down, N left" while respawns are on, as anyone hit may come back.
- A bot coming back under Elimination's overtime flag reacts to it like the others (Custom rules with respawns on).
- Loadout: the right-click menu opens where you clicked, not under the first card.
- Loadout: opened with the Menu key, it hangs under its card and keeps the keyboard there, after Duplicate too.
- "You have the bomb" is no longer told again each time you start and let go of a plant.
- Subway's station announcements are yours only: none play over a bot's train or the match's results, and a new match cuts one still playing.
- Changed: in Attack / Defend the defenders' wait to come back follows the bots' level.
- Against Hard bots it is 1.9 times the field's wait, 25 s at most; against Easy bots three quarters of it, so neither side runs away with the rounds.
- A flag left alone with respawns on slides down whether it is part-raised or fully raised.
