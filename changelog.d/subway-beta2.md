### Subway for everyone
- Subway is now a public map, open to everyone.

### Lifts
- The lift motor is louder, and you hear it for the whole ride.
- Pressing a lift button inside the car shuts the doors at once, and the closing sound plays as you press.
- The doors still never shut on someone standing in the doorway.
- A poster that covered a lift door has moved beside it.

### Subway look
- The gap under the spiral stair is closed, and its wall no longer shows a slit.
- The ticket hall has four more benches.
- The train tunnels curve away into the distance behind a locked steel grille you can't get past, instead of ending in a flat wall.
- The north platform is level to its end, and its ramp is gone.
- The east team climbs out of the tunnels over the platform edge, as everywhere else.
- The tunnel wall no longer flickers round the trains.
- No lines or cracks show on the tiled wall round the tunnel mouths, and underground there is no blue sky in any gap.

### Trains
- The cab fronts are remodelled from reference photos: one tall sweep of dark glass, a glass door and long lamp strips.
- Each outer end has wide, rounded rubber bellows round its door.
