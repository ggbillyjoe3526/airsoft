### Depot's first map models
- Depot's plywood walls are now the game's own model: birch sheets with screwed battens, a rail, skirting and a cap, with low modules under and over the windows.
- Crates on Depot and Woodland are now wooden crates on pine runners with steel edges and stencilled arrows.
- Depot's shipping containers are corrugated containers with door ends, locking bars, hinges and corner castings, still in each container's own paint; the Box is built from container walls and a roof.
- Every map's doors are now two-panel timber doors with a handle and keyhole, set in a lining and architraves, instead of plain green slabs.
