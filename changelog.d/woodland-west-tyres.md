### Woodland's west end has cover
- The lone tyre at the west edge of the Clearing on Woodland is now a stack of tyres you can stand behind.
- The west team has cover going into the Clearing, so the west end wins more Elimination rounds against the east.
- On Woodland an armed bomb now takes 26 seconds to go off instead of 25, giving defenders a little longer to reach it.
- In Plant The Bomb, attacking bots near a dropped bomb no longer pass the job of fetching it back and forth; the bot sent for it now keeps going and picks it up.
