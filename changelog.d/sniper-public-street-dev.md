### Sniper Rifle
- The Sniper Rifle, its M130, M140 and M150 Springs and the Variable Scope are now open to everyone: they unlock at
  their levels (5, 7, 9 and 8), and bots on the other team can carry the Sniper Rifle.

### The Street
- The Street is now dev content: it shows on the Match page only with Settings › Dev › Dev content on, and a match there
  gives no XP and stays out of your records.
