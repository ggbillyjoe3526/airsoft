### Woodland's road stack is lower
- The log stack across the Long road past the Culvert on Woodland is now 1.8 m tall, like the other woodpiles (it was 2.8 m).
- A stand of young spruce now grows behind the stack, where its top metre used to be.
- You still cannot see or shoot along the road from the road's hump to flag B, and you still walk round its south end.
