### Subway lift doors open sooner
- The Subway lift doors start their sound as you press, on the doors' slide, with no soft lead-in.

### What got you is On or Off
- What got you in Settings is now On or Off, On by default; Auto is gone.
- A save set to Off stays Off.

### Tapping Z spots without a stall
- Tapping Z to spot no longer stalls your view: it keeps turning while you tap, so you can track and spot smoothly.
- Holding Z for the spot wheel works as before.

### Levels need more XP
- Each level takes 1.5 times as much XP. A match still gives the same XP.
- Your XP and unlocks are kept; your level number may read lower.

### ALT always sprints
- ALT sprints whenever you move forward: it stands you up, lowers your sight and lets go of walk and lean.
- There is no stamina.

### Red HIT! sign
- The HIT! sign over a hit player is red for both teams.
