### Compact SMG in development
- A new Compact SMG is in development: a compact electric SMG for close quarters, in Settings › Dev › Dev content.
- It fires 18 BBs a second from 45-BB magazines that empty in about 2.5 s, with a quicker draw and reload and 8 % quicker movement than a rifle.
- It moves with half the usual spread and is tighter from the hip, so it shoots well on the run.
- Its 0.20 g BBs at 0.75 J reach about 27 m on target, against 39 m for the AEG Rifle, and the spread is wider with the sight up.
- It has semi and full auto, unlocks at level 10 and has no grips, barrels or extra magazines.
- Some bots carry it in the Breacher kit. It looks like the AEG Rifle for now and uses stand-in sounds.
