### Beta 2 playtest fixes
- Removing a loadout asks in a small panel beside the right-click menu, with Remove and Keep. Esc or a click elsewhere keeps it.
- Focus rings no longer cut through words: on the Remove panel, the Realistic colours switch in Customise and QUIT on the menu rail.
- A quick tap of Z spots the opponent under your crosshair more reliably. A tap can last up to 0.3 s.
- An opponent peeking over a crate or beside a tree is spotted even if your crosshair is on the cover before them. Someone behind a wall still can't be.
- Prompts read the action, then the key in brackets, in capitals: OPEN DOOR [G], CLOSE DOOR [G], CALL LIFT [G], GO TO TICKET HALL [G].
- Vault, climb, reload and bomb prompts: VAULT [SPACE], CLIMB [SPACE], RELOAD [R], PICK UP / PLANT / DEFUSE BOMB [HOLD G].
- Settings › Graphics › Volumetric haze is now On or Off. On with the High and Ultra presets, off with Low and Medium. A saved Custom level reads as On.
