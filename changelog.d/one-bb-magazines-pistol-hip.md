### One BB on every magazine, pistols square at the hip
- Magazines now show one BB at the top instead of a row of three, on the AEG Rifle, the Hi-Cap, the Low-Cap, the GBB Pistol, the Extended and the Pump Shotgun's shell.
- Pistols show one BB on their magazine in the same way instead of two.
- At the hip, the GBB Pistol and Cyber Pistol now lie at the same angle as the rifles, muzzle toward the crosshair, instead of turned a little to the left.
