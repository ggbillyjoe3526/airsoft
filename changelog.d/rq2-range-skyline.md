### Range buildings look like the real thing
- The sheds now have roller shutters, doors, windows, rooflights and gutters.
- The jib cranes have a hoist, a chain, a hook and a cable.
- The phone masts have a three-sided antenna head, dishes and floodlights.
- The water towers have lattice legs, a rounded tank and a caged ladder.
- The silos, the watch tower and the old plane fuselage are rebuilt in more detail.
- The plane fuselage has cockpit windows, torn-open ends and a broken tail.
- The buildings keep their colour in the distance: most sheds are green and the cranes are a brighter yellow.
