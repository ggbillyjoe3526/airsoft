### Lift motor starts on the button
- In Subway, pressing a lift button now starts the lift's motor at once, on a landing or inside the car, instead of a beep.
- Inside the car, the motor rides with you, and the car makes no separate sound as it sets off.
- Pressing the button again restarts the motor rather than playing two at once.
- The button beep and the lift's second motor sound are gone.
- Doors, chime and arrival still play only inside the car. Bots hear lifts as before.
