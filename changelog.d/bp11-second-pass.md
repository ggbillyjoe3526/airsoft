### Subway defenders wait longer against Hard bots
- On Subway in Attack / Defend, defenders now wait about 15 seconds to come back against Hard bots, up from about 13.
- Hard defenders held the poles too well there. Easy and Normal are unchanged.

### Bots keep their jobs
- On Subway in Plant The Bomb, a defender guarding the far site keeps guarding it after being hit and coming back.
- Bots told to Regroup or Follow Me try again after a blocked route, instead of standing still for good.
- With respawns, a teammate following you with Follow Me or Regroup falls in behind you again after coming back.

### Respawns at full forward spawns
- With every forward spawn taken, you come back at your start instead of inside a teammate.
- Someone on the floor above or below a spawn spot no longer blocks it.

### Bomb and round end
- End the round in the Dev tab while the bomb is being armed no longer lets the arming finish and run the fuse.
- A round that ends while the bomb is being armed no longer leaves the planter frozen through the pause between rounds.

### Menus, loading and rounds
- The vault prompt shows the new Jump key after a rebind from the pause menu.
- Pressing Esc or switching away on the last moment of the loading screen no longer leaves it frozen.
- The Match page remembers a field's mode after a visit to the Range.
- Skipping rounds no longer makes the screen reader read out every hit.

### Typed values in data tables
- Map data accepts a typed minus sign, such as −5.
- Level data refuses "50 %" as a multiplier.
