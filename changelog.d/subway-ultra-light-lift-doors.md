### Subway lit on Ultra, lift doors heard before they move
- On Ultra, Subway's ceilings, shaded corners and escalator shafts are no longer near black, and they no longer brighten only as you walk up to them. Ultra fills the station's shade as High does and keeps its lamp shadows and haze.
- A lift's doors are now heard a moment before they start to shut. The car still leaves at the same time.
- An arriving lift is now heard opening, with its chime. Its doors used to slide open silently.
- Standing off to the side of a lift's landing no longer muffles its doors.
