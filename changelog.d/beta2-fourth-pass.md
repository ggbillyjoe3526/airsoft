### Crouch holds when you sprint on the spot
- With crouch on toggle, pressing Alt while standing still no longer stands you up, so Alt+Tab or Alt+Enter behind cover keeps you crouched.
- Running forward with Alt still stands you up.

### Pings land after a Z tap
- A middle-click ping straight after a Z tap now lands instead of being dropped.

### Enter can't be bound
- Enter and keypad Enter can't be bound to an action any more, since Alt+Enter switches fullscreen.
- A saved binding on Enter goes back to that action's default.

### Smaller window limit
- The game window can't be dragged smaller than 960 x 540.

### Bots and floors
- Bots no longer count a teammate on another floor, such as Subway's concourse above the platform, as already in a fight.
- They still come to a fight on the platform under them.

### Lift doors close from half open
- A lift's doors that start shutting from half open, with someone in the doorway, now close from where they stand.

### GBB Pistol steadier on the move
- The GBB Pistol is tighter on the move, so hip-fired on the run it is now tighter than the Cyber Pistol (1.19° against 1.26°).
- The Cyber Pistol stays tighter standing.

### Compact SMG and Pump Shotgun models
- The Compact SMG and Pump Shotgun no longer swap to a simpler model at distance on Low.
- Ultra's lighting treats them like the other replicas. No visible change in normal play.

### Loading picture resizes smoothly
- The loading screen's picture shrinks smoothly in a small window.
