### Lift sounds heard from inside
- Pressing a lift button from just inside the doorway now shuts the doors straight away, heard right after the press, and you ease back from the doors as they shut. Before, the doors waited silently until you stepped further in.
- Pressing another floor while the doors are still opening shuts them again at once.
- A lift's doors, chime and buzzer are now heard only from inside its car.
- Outside, you hear only the lift's buttons being pressed, and the minimap and sound cues show only those presses.
