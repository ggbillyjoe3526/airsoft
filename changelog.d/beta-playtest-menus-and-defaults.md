### Quicker menus
- The Match, Loadout, Armory and Settings tabs open at once, as their pages are kept ready.
- Picking an interface colour in Settings › Display › Interface › Colour now shows at once. It used to take a few seconds.
- The Graphics tab fits on one page, with nothing to scroll.

### New defaults
- Gauge patterns are off by default, and HUD opacity starts at 60 %. A save on the old defaults moves to the new ones.
- The frame-rate limit is a slider from 30 to 360, with an Off button beside it. Off is the default.
- New Tooltips switch under Display › Interface › Colour, on by default. Off hides the notes that pop up on hover.

### Loadout menu
- Right-click a loadout, or press Shift+F10, for Rename, Duplicate, Replace and Remove.
- These are no longer links on the right of the loadout strip.

### Tidier screens
- Clicking a level on the Armory's level line no longer draws a blue ring round it.
- Customise's 3/4 view has no circle under the replica.
- On the Match page, the Your team cards have no colour line down the left. The picked team's card is edged in its colour (blue Alpha, orange Beta).
- The pause menu's Timed challenge switch no longer overlaps its words.

### Quieter HUD notes
- The vault and climb prompt is just your Jump key in capitals, such as SPACE.
- When an order ends by itself, no "Back to the team plan" notice shows.
- The round clock reads 0:00 once a round is over (it could read 0:02).
- YOU HAVE THE BOMB shows for a moment as you pick the bomb up, then goes.
