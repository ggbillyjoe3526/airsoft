# game/play

A match, played: `PlaySession` builds the level, steps the simulation once a physics tick with the player's command
and hands the tick's events on; everything else here draws the state every rendered frame, between the last two ticks
(docs/ARCHITECTURE.md › Contracts 1 and 3). Nothing here writes simulation state.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `play_session.gd` | `PlaySession`: the map, the simulation, the views, built in four steps (map, level, teams, views; one a frame while `staged`, reported by `progressed` for the loading screen, then `started` once drawn); you play `player_team` (Alpha or Beta); Esc and focus loss pause (the pause menu names the map, mode, round and seed); `left` back to the title; the smoke test's autopilot; a counted match's XP goes to the HUD (`Hud.show_xp`) when it is settled; `practice`: the range alone with its targets, no bots or rounds, the timed challenge (`set_challenge`, switched on the pause menu and saved as `Settings.practice_challenge`) keeping its best in Records; the HUD's readout and challenge display come from `Hud.setup_range`; the Loadout key (`loadout`) or the pause menu's row sends `loadout_requested`, `practice_spot` takes where you stood and `resume` puts you back there on the range built again |
| `practice_spot.gd` | `PracticeSpot`: where you stood on the range (place, view, stance, slot, holster, challenge switch, seed), taken when the Loadout opens and given back to the new range |
| `aim_follow.gd` | `AimFollow`: the sight's raise as drawn, following the simulation's at its own raise speed over the simulation time each frame stands for, so it comes up evenly at any game speed (FB6) |
| `first_person_view.gd` | `FirstPersonView`: the camera at the leaned eye (`Lean.offset`), rolled a little in a lean and eased out of one a wall cuts short, dipped and tipped over a vault, turned by the mouse every frame, lifted by recoil (eased over simulation time), zoomed by the drawn sight (`AimFollow`); Reduced motion draws less roll, dip and tip, never less recoil |
| `torch_beams.gd` | `TorchBeams`: everyone else's lit torch as a cone, a lens glare and a lit spot, three instanced draws for the match; your own as a haze on Low, or by `FirstPersonView`'s real spot from the rail on Medium and up while your kit carries one (shadows on High and Ultra with Sun shadows on) |
| `laser_beams.gd` | `LaserBeams`: Dev › Lasers' beams (LZ1), one instanced draw of camera-facing ribbons in the team colours from each muzzle to where its laser stopped: a flash a single or semi shot, three a burst, held on through full auto |
| `replica_view.gd` | `ReplicaView`: the replica in hand from its model: hip and sights, draw, the holster, sprint carry, reload cant and clip, kick, sway, pulled back from walls, a gas pistol's slide locked back on empty (`SlideLockPose`), the fire selector at its mode (`SelectorPose`), in the Kit's paint for its slot, with its parts; aimed along an optic's sight line; Reduced motion draws less sway, kick and reload cant; inspected (IN1), turned over by its `InspectPose` while its Inspect clip plays, its parts' sounds sent as `inspect_sound` |
| `motion_scale.gd` | `MotionScale`: how much of each movement the view, the replica, the wind dust and the HUD's pulse draw: all of it, or with Reduced motion `data/accessibility.md`'s shares (`MotionScale.of`); built on a settings change |
| `replica_parts.gd`, `iron_sights.gd` | `ReplicaParts`: a replica's parts fitted from their models (`assets/models/replicaParts/`), the one way the hands, the figures and the menu pictures do it: the standard magazine or flash hider replaced, a longer barrel carrying the muzzle out, the iron sights hidden whole under any optic (`IronSights`: every bone attachment on a bone ending in `Sight`) |
| `optic_glass.gd`, `optic_glass.gdshader` | `OpticGlass`: the red dot's and the scope's glass in hand, the view through it drawn by a second camera without your replica (the scope's magnified, only inside its glass), with the lit dot or the reticle; draws only while a sight is raised, its picture following the graphics' render scale and edge smoothing |
| `bb_view.gd`, `puff_view.gd` | BBs in the air (glowing ones green with a streak, two more instanced draws: WL8) and the puffs where they land (a big one of a prop's dust where someone runs into it; a green one and leaves falling to the ground where a BB stops in a bush, a leaf or two where one comes out of an edge), each one MultiMesh; `PuffView.disc` is every puff's soft disc |
| `flag_view.gd`, `flag_cloth.gdshader` | `FlagView`: Attack / Defend's pole at the map's flag spot, visual only (no collider): the flag climbing the rope with the progress in the attackers' colour, waving in a shader, and the reach ring coloured by who works the rope (white idle, attackers raising, defenders lowering, yellow contested); the cloth shader's chequer for the overtime flag |
| `door_view.gd`, `door_leaf.gdshader` | `DoorView`: the hinged doors' leaves as one MultiMesh swinging as `GameState.doors` has them, in the shared light; on a baked map each face takes the baked light of the wall beside its jamb on its own side (`relight`, BP7: instance colour and custom data), so a door into a dim room is dim on its side |
| `overtime_flag_view.gd` | `OvertimeFlagView`: Elimination's overtime flag at the map's spot, visual only (no collider): built once a match, hidden until it goes up, rising out of the ground in 0.8 s (at once under Reduced motion), a short pole with a chequered off-white and dark cloth turned to the view, and its 1 m reach ring (off-white, yellow contested); both take the toucher's colour once touched |
| `grenade_view.gd` | `GrenadeView`: the smoke and flash bombs (SF1), made once a match: a canister per slot of `GameState.grenades` (green smoke, violet flash; spinning in flight, on its side at rest), each smoke's cloud as 12 to 28 soft puffs in its ellipsoid by the Mesh detail (fewer and larger on Low; one MultiMesh, proximity-faded where they meet the ground, dimmed by night), a flash's bang as a 0.3 s light and flare, the bomb in your hand while the pin comes out, and a wash over the screen: white while a flash dazzles the eyes the view is through (fading over the last 1.2 s; at most 0.6 with Reduced motion), the smoke's colour while they are inside a cloud |
| `mag_toss_view.gd` | `MagTossView`: the magazine hand-off's magazines in the air (MH3), one copy per slot of `GameState.mag_tosses` made once a match: the thrower's replica's own magazine mesh (`FigureModels.magazine_mesh`; a dark box without one) from the hand to the catcher's chest, arcing 0.1 m a metre up to 0.6 m and spinning 2.5 turns a second |
| `range_targets_view.gd`, `range_view.gd` | Practice on the range: `RangeTargetsView` draws `GameState.targets` in greybox (RANGE_VISUALS): white plates on posts that swing back when hit, plywood figures the size of the hit volume falling back and rising, a mover's rails and trolley, a pop-up's motor box, a swinger's frame and arm, the challenge's lit glow and START plate; `RangeView` the floor lines and wall boards at each marked distance |
| `range_pilot.gd` | `RangePilot`: the smoke test's pilot in practice, shooting the nearest still plate |
| `figure_view.gd` | `FigureView`: another player as its rigged figure (`FigureBody`, `figures/`; every bot, no switch since FI7), placed and posed between ticks, its replicas in its team's paint with their parts' figure copies handed to the hands, "HIT!" while calling a hit, the fade and the hit flinch; its replica's muzzle for the gas puffs; where the sun casts no shadows (`GraphicsApply.contact_shadows`) a soft disc on the floor under it (one shared mesh), held on the floor through a jump |
| `figures/` | The rigged figures (step 23): `FigureModels` (each kind loaded once a session), `FigureSidecar`, `FigureBody` (one figure: its head, levels, shadow twin, team paint, fade), `FigureMix` (who is a robot); see its README |
| `replica_paint.gd` | `ReplicaPaint`: a `ReplicaSchemes` paint put on a replica model's Body, Furniture, Detail, Accent and Metal surfaces (the Cyber Pistol keeps its own, plain grey with no glow under Realistic colours); one shared material set per paint, built once; the paint a bot wears by team |
| `selector_pose.gd` | `SelectorPose`: holds a replica model's fire selector where its Selector clip puts the fire mode, over whichever clip plays (a skeleton modifier) |
| `slide_lock_pose.gd` | `SlideLockPose`: holds a gas pistol's slide back where its SlideLock clip leaves it while locked, over whichever clip plays, the Reload clip too (a skeleton modifier) |
| `sim_audio.gd` | `SimAudio`: every sound from the events (a replica's by its sound profile: its power's, or its own, the Cyber Pistol's) (a vault's hands and kit too; a run into a noisy prop as its `prop_<sound>` cue out to `prop_reach`; steps on gravel and steel plate louder and further by their carry; on the range steel ringing, figures knocking, your target hits confirmed, the challenge's countdown and Go beeps), positional for others, plain for you (your own hit's tick on Interface, clear of the field's dip); a landing louder and lower the harder it comes down (from `FootstepConfig.land_min_speed` to `hard_land_speed`), with a kit rustle under a hard one; kit rustle from `FoleyTracker`; the referee's whistle from `WhistleQueue` on one Interface voice; the map's ambience (`MapData.ambience`, the yard with no map): its day beds looping on Ambience, one unpanned copy each, and its calls from `AmbientCalls`, positional on Ambience at the pool's lowest priority; others' only within reach of the ears the view is through (steps 22 m and 4 per 0.1 s, rustle 9 m and 3 per 0.15 s, impacts 8 per 0.1 s, the range's targets 80 m, the rest 60 m); voices made once and shared through `VoicePool`s by priority; a cue never plays its last variant again; pitch and level spread by kind (none on interface cues); others' sounds and BB impacts muffled through walls by `SoundOcclusion`; each bot callout (`CalloutLog`) as the radio click, a teammate's in your ear panned toward them on its own voices, an enemy's within 15 m from where they stand, muffled; none with Settings › HUD › Callouts off |
| `foley_tracker.gd` | `FoleyTracker`: kit rustle from the state (the browser's foley.ts): a crouch, a stand or a lean away from upright starting, once per movement, for anyone in play; leaning back is quiet; no event, so bots never hear it; records made once a match |
| `whistle_queue.gd` | `WhistleQueue`: the referee's blasts waiting their turn in a fixed queue: two short start a round, one long ends it (and the range's Time), three more end the match (`SoundConfig`'s pattern); a new round's whistle cuts what is left |
| `ambient_calls.gd` | `AmbientCalls`: a field's calls, each kind on its own seeded gaps at a random bearing and its distance round the listener, at its height; seeded from the call's `rng_seed` and the match's, on the simulation's clock, afresh each match |
| `sound_occlusion.gd` | `SoundOcclusion`: muffling through walls from the listener's eyes (`SoundPath`'s knee and head rays to a character, one ray stopping 0.2 m short of a BB impact): open, or the half or full occlusion bus at the browser's level for the share (a silenced shot on the lower corner, at both levels); a share reused for 6 ticks, at most 24 rays a tick (over it, the last share; an impact open), none beyond 60 m where the share closes to full over 60 m more. On the smoke match: at most 5 rays a tick, 0.19 on average, 1.3 µs a tick (60 µs at most) |
| `voice_pool.gd` | `VoicePool`: katanashotgun's pool over the voices made at setup (a cue at its max reuses its oldest, else a free voice, else steals the oldest of the lowest priority no higher than its own, else drops and counts); `VoicePool.StartLimit`, the browser game's at most so many starts a window |
| `sound_mix.gd` | `SoundMix`: the Field bus muffled and turned down while you are out of play (until the match is over) and dipped under your hit and the referee's whistle (a round's or the match's end, the range's Time), shaped by a plain `SoundMix.DuckEnvelope` every frame; left at 0 dB with its filter off when it leaves play; sets the occlusion buses' corners and levels. Buses (`default_bus_layout.tres`): Field (Effects, with Footsteps, Power, Muffled (a silenced shot, low-passed), EffectsHalf and EffectsFull under it; Ambience) and Interface under Master; PowerHalf and PowerFull under Power; Master, Effects, Power, Ambience and Interface have a volume in Settings |
| `wind_dust.gd` | `WindDust`: specks of dust round the eye drifting with the match's breeze, one GPU particle box; each adds only the light falling on it (its own shader, no ambient), so it glints in sunlight and is gone in shade, and it fades out near the eye; the graphics set how many and Reduced motion takes its share of that; none hides it |
| `drift_puffs.gd`, `gas_puffs.gd`, `kicked_dust.gd` | `DriftPuffs`: a fixed pool of soft puffs that move, grow, fade and keep under a height cap, one MultiMesh hidden while none is in the air, nothing allocated a puff. `GasPuffs`: a gas replica's breath, a puff from the muzzle and a smaller one from the port each shot, yours from the hands, a bot's from its figure's replica in hand. `KickedDust`: a faint puff of the map's dust (`MapData.kicked_dust_tint`) at a sprinting step and a bigger one on landing within 35 m, its top under 0.3 m, off where the wind dust is 0 % |
| `field_lighting.gd` | `FieldLighting`: the sun (or the moon), the sky (by night its own: stars and the moon's disc) and the environment, with the night's haze; the graphics switch the sun's shadows, their range and ambient occlusion in place (GraphicsApply.apply_field) |
| `light_pools.gd` | `LightPools`: by night, the map's `lights` (fires and lanterns) as drawn pools on the ground and glows in one mesh each, with real OmniLight3D lights on the nearest few (the Mesh detail row's count, or 8 on Lamp light Far), casting shadows with Lamp light on; fires flicker, still under Reduced motion; null by day or on a map with none |
| `skyline_view.gd`, `skyline_shapes.gd` | `SkylineView`: the map's skyline (`MapData.skyline`), the pieces some player sees over the walls (SkylineSight) as one vertex-coloured mesh in the shared light faded toward the horizon, casting no shadow (a piece seen only past a nearer solid one left out), no collider, and the chimneys' smoke as one particle system from Medium (`smokes`), bent by the wind and frozen under Reduced motion; null on a map with none. `SkylineShapes`: each kind (shed, water tower, crane, containers, chimney, silo, power line, hill, watch tower, fuselage, mast, treeline) as swept parts, and the points a sight line aims at |
| `site/site_surface.gd`, `site/site_surface.gdshader`, `site/site_sky.gdshader` | `SiteSurface`: the site dress's one material (DD1) and the per-vertex box and finish it reads; procedural canvases in world space from two tiling noise textures and two Poly Haven photo sets, the bevel highlight, the map's puddles as a mask; `low` on Low mesh detail. The day sky with soft cumulus |
| `roof_view.gd` | `RoofView`: a roofed map's ceiling and trusses (one unshaded mesh) and its lamps (one MultiMesh), the meshes casting no shadow (RoofLamps' lights do, on High and Ultra), from `RoofConfig` (`PlayTuning.roof`); null for a map open to the sky. Its collider is MapLevel's |
| `roof_lamps.gd` | `RoofLamps`: a roof's lamps as real lights (Graphics › Lamp light, UG1), a shadowed SpotLight3D under each, every lamp lit over the whole roof on High and Ultra, shadowed near (High) or all the way (Ultra) by the engine's distance fade, off on Low and Medium; `key_share` and `ambient_share` put the sun out and turn the sky's light down under the roof while they shine (UG2). Built by RoofView, nothing per frame |
| `site/site_dress.gd` | `SiteDress`: a site-dressed map's details (container frames, crate battens, caps, coping, the skip's rim, the watch tower's net and flag, shade sails) as one mesh in the site material, its sandbags as one MultiMesh, its zone stencils; `dress_sky` (day only); null without a site dress |
| `map_occluders.gd` | `MapOccluders`: the map's tall solid plain blocks (`MapStyle.occluder_kinds`, no ramp or prop, tall and long enough) as one ArrayOccluder3D, each box inset; the root viewport culls what they hide with Settings › Graphics › Occlusion culling on (Medium and up, `GraphicsApply.apply`); null on a map with none |
| `bush_view.gd` | `BushView`: a map's bushes (WL5) as placeholder ellipsoids, every one in one instanced draw on every preset, a seeded pick of greens; null without any |
| `woods_view.gd`, `woods_shapes.gd` | `WoodsView`: a wood's look in placeholders (WL4): every tree's crown (instanced, every preset), and from Medium the forest beyond the fence, grass tufts in 32 m tiles, the puddles and mud (drawn first of the see-through), the leaves, fallen wood and moss; by night the fireflies and motes, the Wind dust share, still under Reduced motion; no collider; null without any. `WoodsShapes`: where each part goes and its colour, seeded, as plain data (`Placed`) and meshes |
| `tick_pacer.gd` | `TickPacer`: how many fixed ticks each physics tick runs at the Dev settings' game speed (or the smoke test's speed-up), and the drawing fraction between them |
| `spectate.gd` | `Spectate`: the next bot in play Spectate any bot follows |
| `minimap_hearing.gd` | `MinimapHearing`: what your side heard for the minimap, fed each tick's events through the ears the view is through: one patch per enemy, the last footstep, vault, run into a noisy prop (as a step, within the knock's reach) or shot heard within its reach (a silencer halves a shot's, loud ground lengthens a step's), off their real place by a blur on the match's minimap stream (`Seeds.minimap_seed`) with them always inside it, renewed in place while they stay in it, fading after its life; a hit call removes theirs, a new round clears all; `beyond_view` for the rim. Plain data for the HUD (`PlaySession.minimap_hearing`) |
| `sound_cues.gd` | `SoundCues`: Settings › Accessibility's sound cues, fed each tick's events while they are on, through the same ears as `MinimapHearing`: the other team's steps and vaults within `SoundConfig.step_reach` (times a step's carry) and their runs into noisy props as steps within the knock's reach, anyone's shot but the listener's within `max_distance` × the replica's heard_scale, anyone's hit call but the listener's within `max_distance` (walls not checked); one marker per player and kind from a pool of `AccessibilityConfig.cue_markers` (the oldest makes way), its life, fade and opacity by distance; a new round clears them; `bearing` for the ring. Plain data `HudSoundCues` draws (`PlaySession.sound_cues`) |
| `bot_intent_view.gd` | `BotIntentView`: the Dev settings' Bot intentions, a label over each bot saying what it is doing |
| `retro_pixels.gd`, `retro_pixels.gdshader` | `RetroPixels`: the Dev settings' Retro pixels, a full-screen filter under the HUD |
| `frame_watch.gd` | `FrameWatch`: the automatic graphics step-down's watch (the browser game's, DisplayConfig's numbers): fed each frame's time while the graphics are the automatic pick, focused, unpaused, in a live round of a match once its shaders are warm and not on the simpler renderer; two slow 2 s windows in a row (over 5 % of frames past 20 ms, or 1.25 frames of the limit) ask once a match; `PlaySession` steps the pick one preset down for the run at the round's end (`GraphicsApply.pick_for_run`, never saved) and the HUD says so for 5 s; Low never steps; a pause, focus loss or a player's own pick starts it over; the Dev settings' Pretend slow frames counts every frame slow |
| `shader_prewarm.gd` | `ShaderPrewarm`: draws the late materials (every figure material solid and fading, a BB, a puff, the sign) as specks for the first frames, so nothing compiles mid-match |

You play the team the Match page picked (Alpha by default) with bot teammates against bots on the other. Tuning:
`PlayTuning` (`game/config/play_tuning.tres`). Tests: `tests/unit/test_play_session.gd`; the gas puffs, kicked dust,
sunlit specks and contact shadows `tests/unit/test_dust_effects.gd`; the step-down `tests/unit/test_frame_watch.gd`;
sound `tests/unit/test_voice_pool.gd`, `tests/unit/test_sound_mix.gd`, `tests/unit/test_sound_occlusion.gd`,
`tests/unit/test_sound_path.gd`, `tests/unit/test_foley_tracker.gd`, `tests/unit/test_whistle_queue.gd`,
`tests/unit/test_ambient_calls.gd`, landings in `tests/unit/test_footsteps.gd`.
