# Plant The Bomb's models

The bomb (`bomb.glb`) and the wire cutters that defuse it (`cutters.glb`), built from the owner's photos by
`tools/blender/bomb/bomb.py` (PB2), unbranded and non-working. `game/play/plant_view.gd` draws them: the bomb on the
ground, at its carrier's hip and armed in a site, its `Lamp` tinted by its state; the cutters at its red wire while
someone defuses it. How to make them: [docs/CC0_ASSETS.md](../../../docs/CC0_ASSETS.md) › Plant The Bomb's models.
Record every file in [docs/ASSETS.md](../../../docs/ASSETS.md).
